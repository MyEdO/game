# <span id="page-22-0" data-folio="23"></span>**1. SPECIES**

**A)** Choose from **Human**, **Dwarf**, **Halfling**, **High Elf**, or **Wood Elf** *or*

Roll on the **Species Table**, and gain an extra point of Fortune if you accept the first result.

**SPECIES TABLE**

| D100  | Species            |
|-------|--------------------|
| 01–90 | Human (page 26)    |
| 91–94 | Halfling (page 28) |
| 95–98 | Dwarf (page 30)    |
| 99    | High Elf (page 32) |
| 00    | Wood Elf (page 34) |

**B)** Copy the following information onto your Character Sheet from the section detailing your Species:

**Age and Appearance:** decide on your Character's age and appearance. Options are presented for inspiration if you get stuck.

**Languages:** write your language Skills into the 'Known Languages' section of your Character Sheet. Languages are a Skill that uses the Intelligence Characteristic — there's an example of how to do so the next page (page 24), and you'll learn how to determine you Characteristics on page 38.

**Skills:** choose your Species Skills now and mark them on your Character Sheet, or make a note of them and decide after determining your Career Skill options and Characteristics.

**Movement, Fate and Fortune:** copy these numbers to your sheet.

**Talents:** choose your Species Talents and write them on your Character Sheet with a summary of their effects (see pages 114–128). If you are awarded a Random Talent, determine it by rolling D100 on the table below. If you roll a Talent you already have, re-roll.
