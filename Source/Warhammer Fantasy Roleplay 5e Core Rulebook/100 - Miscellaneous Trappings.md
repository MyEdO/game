**Bandage:** Removes 1 *Bleeding* Condition. This uses up the bandage.

**Bedroll:** Endurance Tests rolled to resist Cold Exposure (see page 170) gain a bonus of +2 SL when resting.

**Candle:** Provides illumination for 2 yards when lit.

**Feinkopf Lamp:** A safety lamp emitting the light of a candle. The lamp was developed for Reikland's mines by Engineer Commander Feinkopf. It flares brightly in 'firedamp' (explosive gasses); after 1d10 Rounds of exposure to the lamp, the firedamp will explode. It is wise to withdraw before this happens.

**Grappling Hook:** Coupled with a rope, allows unscalable surfaces to be climbed.

**Instrument:** Various instruments are included in this category. The standard price and Encumbrance reflects medium-sized instruments (e.g. mandolin, coach horn, small drum). Small instruments are half the price and 0 Encumbrance Points (e.g. flute, recorder, tambourine). Larger instruments are double the price and 2 Encumbrance Points (e.g. harp, lute, large drum).

**Lamp Oil:** Contains enough fuel to light a torch for 1 hour, a lantern for 4 hours, or a Davrich lamp for 8 hours.

**Lantern:** Provides illumination for 10 yards.

**Storm Lantern:** Shutters protect the flame from wind, and also enable the light to be directed in a 90° arc or darkened altogether. Provides illumination for 10 yards, or 20 when targeted.

**Tent:** A medium-sized tent accommodating four people sleeping in close quarters. Small tents accommodate 2 people for half the price and 1 Encumbrance Point. Large tents accommodate 8 people for double the price and 4 Encumbrance Points.

**Torch:** Illuminates 10 yards. Counts as an Improvised Weapon, although a Critical Hit inflicts 1 *Ablaze* Condition in addition to all other effects.
