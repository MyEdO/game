# <span id="page-350-0" data-folio="351"></span>**THE LOATHSOME RATMEN**

Skaven are a malevolent species of ratmen eking out a foul existence in the sewers and tunnels beneath the Empire's cities, watching, waiting, and plotting. So rarely are they seen that those who do glimpse them usually dismiss them as beastmen or mutants. Few suspect the truth: an Under-Empire stretches beneath the Old World, its tunnels linking every major city and beyond.

Skaven society is built upon the labour of captives taken from across the Old World, and their network of spies and informants is vast. The unscrupulous supply prisoners and warpstone to the skaven in exchange for secret knowledge and other unsavoury favours.

The skaven know their survival depends upon secrecy and protect it ruthlessly. Those foolish enough to speak openly of a civilisation of intelligent ratmen beneath the Empire's cobbled streets may soon be found dead in a gutter, the unfortunate victim of an unlikely accident.

*'I never seen nothin'. There were no ratmen, you hear? Just bad luck. Wilbur slipped and fell, that's all. He got careless, fell down a ladder onto his own knife. Ten times. Just bad luck.'*

— Kristiana Fellger, retired Sewer Jack

# **Clanrat**

Most skaven are clanrats, hailing from one of the many complex skaven clans that constantly bicker, politic, backstab, and eventually war on each other. They generally act on the orders of higher-status skaven, but are always looking for a way to secure a better position, most often by betrayal. They usually dress in mouldering leather or filthy cloth, with scraps of rusty and tarnished metal serving as makeshift armour. Clanrats are often sent out as scouts or raiders to scavenge for goods, search for warpstone, or raid for slaves.

# <span id="page-351-0" data-folio="352"></span>**Stormvermin**

The elite fighters of the skaven are the stormvermin: bigger, stronger, tougher, and more disciplined than clanrats. They will serve as the core of any major assault and comprise the bodyguard of important skaven. Stormvermin are usually well armed and armoured, sporting weapon combinations favoured by their clan.

The Stormvermin has been created by adding the Elite template (see page 353) to the basic Clanrat profile.

# **Rat Ogre**

Rat ogres are hulking brutes, bred in the dark caverns of the Under-empire by the demented ministerings of Clan Moulder's master mutators. They are stupid, but when driven by their skaven masters are fearless and unrelenting in combat. Rarely encountered on their own, they tend to accompany ranking skaven as bodyguards.
