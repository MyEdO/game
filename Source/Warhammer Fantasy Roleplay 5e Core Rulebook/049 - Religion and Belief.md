# <span id="page-201-0" data-folio="202"></span>• **RELIGION AND BELIEF** •

Signs of religion are everywhere in the Old World, from the magnificent temples in the great cities to the humble wayside shrines and household altars. Old Worlders do their best to stay on the right side of their gods.

High-ranking clerics are wealthy and powerful, wielding as much influence and prestige as the greatest nobles. At the other end of the scale, village priests tend to the spiritual needs of rural communities, preaching the tenets of their faith and interceding with their patron deities on behalf of their flocks. Several faiths also support templar orders, private armies of knights who answer to the head of their faith rather than a noble or elector count, which can cause significant political friction.
