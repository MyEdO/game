# <span id="page-27-0" data-folio="28"></span>**DWARFS**

Dwarfs are tough, resourceful, proud, and honourable. Given their long memories and proud natures, they bear grudges against those who have dishonoured them, even taking on the grievances of their ancestors. They believe their forebears watch over them, nursing bitterness long after death. Whilst it might be hard to win a dwarf 's friendship, once given, it is sincere and lasting.

They are squat and powerfully built, with thick hair and heavy features. The length of a dwarf 's beard is a mark of pride and status, and to shave it is a terrible shame. While not as ageless as the near-immortal elves, dwarfs can live for many centuries. Indeed, some say that as long as a dwarf has a purpose, they will not die unless struck down in battle.

Dwarfs are encountered throughout the Old World, although most reside in their holds, vast fortified mines dug deep beneath the mountains. For all their prowess in battle and expertise in craftsmanship, their civilisation is a shadow of its former glory and continues to decline. They often speak their own language, khazalid, but are reluctant to teach it to others. They use the name Karaz Ankor — meaning Everlasting Realm — to refer both to their ancient mountain empire and to the beleaguered holds that remain under dwarf control. The dwarf calendar begins in the year –4523 IC, when the Great Hall of Karaz-a-Karak was first established, and dwarfs still pay respect to the high king who sits upon its throne, though for those living in the Empire, he is a distant presence.

# **Opinions**

#### **On Reiklander Humans…**

*'Like my father's father's father's father, I've lived in the Reikland all my life. As folk go, they know not to mess with my business and show me the respect I deserve, as is right. Yes, they're unreliable, and as changeable as the wind, but they're also resourceful and shrewd, so I'd recommend them as business partners, as they see solutions I'd not even consider.'*

— Garral Herraksson, Jeweller from Eilhart

#### **On Halflings…**

*'I've heard they make sharp archers and nimble scouts, and that their culinary art makes folk grateful for their company. Well, a slice of chuff and a pint of ale keep me happy, and the halflings are too immature and vulnerable to adapt to life beyond their farms and pie shops.'*

— Bardin Hargirson, Mercenary from Karak Azgaraz

#### **On High Elves…**

*'Those lackbeards once raised their towers where Marienburg and Altdorf now stand before their great betrayal of our ancestors, the atrocities that led to the War of Vengeance. Well, we showed them, so now you barely see them hereabouts. Grudges may have been settled, but still, don't trust them.'*

— Dern Ketilsson, Diplomat from Karak Azgaraz

#### **On Wood Elves…**

*'On the far side of the mountains lies a benighted forest where no sensible folk travel. The trees there remember the bite of axes and are vindictive in their punishment of those who bear them. These wood spirits keep a clan of elves in their thrall, and many a dwarf of the Grey Mountains has suffered the sting of their arrows.'*

— Rungni Garilsson, Scholar from Karak Norn

#### **Options: Animosity (Elves)**

Given the long memories of dwarfs and their tendency to bear grudges, many harbour a deep resentment towards elves for their part in the War of Vengeance. As such, many dwarfs have the Animosity (Elves) Psychology trait (see page 183 and 356). Given some WFRP parties contain both elves and dwarfs, this may lead to unhelpful tensions. So, you may prefer to ignore this Talent between party members.

#### <span id="page-28-0" data-folio="29"></span>**Names**

Dwarfs usually identify with their clan but, particularly in the case of those who live to the north, may prefer to identify as the son (sson), daughter (dottir), niece (niz), or nephew (nev) of another dwarf. Others might choose a grand-sounding title reflecting a notable feat or deed.

**Dwarf Forenames:** Alaeric, Bori, Bardlin, Belorn, Ergrim, Fenna, Galazil (Golden Haired), Gnoldok (Wise Eyes), Grufbad, Gottri, Gudrin, Kargrim, Okri (Crafter), Thymbrin, Ulfar

**Dwarf Surnames:** Alriksniz or Alriksnev, Anvilhalm, Bardinsniz or Bardinsnev, Gnoldoksniz or Gnoldoksnev, Goreksson or Goreksdottir, Gottrisson or Gottrisdottir, Gudrunsniz or Gudrunsnev, Okrisniz or Okrisnev, Orc-render, Snorrisson or Snorrisdottir, Stoutbelly, Sturnfast, Ulfarsson or Ulfarsdottir

**Clan Names:** Ardrungan (Reikland), Dokkintroll (Grey Mountains), Ganvalgger (Grey Mountains), Gromheld (Reikland), Gunnisson (Silver Mountain), Harrazlings (Reikland), Helhein (Ekrund), Svengeln (World's Edge Mountains), Threkkson (Dragonback Mountains), Thrungtak (Grey Mountains), Unboki (Reikland), Wyrgrinti (Grey Mountains)

#### **Dwarf Physical Characteristics**

**Age:** 15+10d10

**Height:** 4'3"+1d10"

**Eye colour:** Coal, Lead, Steel, Blue, Earth Brown, Bronze, Hazel, Green, Copper, Gold

**Hair colour:** White, Grey, Pale Blond, Golden, Copper, Bronze, Brown, Dark Brown, Reddish Brown, Black

### **Fluent Languages**

Take six Advances (+30) in both the Language (Khazalid) and Language (Reikspiel) Skills

### **Starting Skills**

Add one Advance (+5) to any five of the following Skills: Consume Alcohol, Cool, Endurance, Entertain (Storytelling), Evaluate, Intimidate, Lore (Dwarfs), Lore (Geology), Lore (Metallurgy), Melee (Basic), Trade (any one)

### **Starting Talents**

*Magic Resistance*, *Night Vision*, *Read/Write* or *Relentless*, *Resolute* or *Strong-minded*, *Sturdy*

#### **Fate**

Dwarfs have had their heyday, and are in decline. Dwarf Characters start with 2 Fate.

#### **Fortune**

Nobody would call a dwarf lucky, least of all a dwarf. They start with 2 Fortune.

#### **Movement**

Dwarfs move with a relentless stride, not a rapid one. Dwarfs have a Movement rating of 3.
