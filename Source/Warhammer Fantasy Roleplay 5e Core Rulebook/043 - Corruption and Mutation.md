# <span id="page-186-0" data-folio="187"></span>**CORRUPTION AND MUTATION**

Few threats in the Old World are more insidious than the Ruinous Powers. These malign forces hunger to corrupt your mind, warp your flesh, and claim your soul. Corruption Points represent your slow descent into Chaos. Whenever you are exposed to corruption, you may gain Corruption Points. The more you accumulate, the closer you come to damnation, until, eventually, you begin to change…

# **Gaining Corruption Points**

Corruption Points are gained through exposure to places, people, or objects tainted by Chaos, or to situations especially favoured by the Dark Gods.

You do not Test for Corruption every time you are exposed. Instead, at the end of the session, the GM determines your greatest degree of exposure and calls for a single Corruption Test.

Multiple exposures, or exposure to multiple sources, may increase the Test's Difficulty, change the Characteristic Tested, or increase the Corruption gained on a failure, at the GM's discretion. The stronger the Corrupting Influence, the more SL you must score to avoid gaining Corruption Points.

> A Corruption Test is a **Challenging (+0 SL) Endurance or Cool** Test, as determined by the GM. Physical corruption is usually resisted with Endurance, while spiritual corruption is resisted with Cool. The stronger the Corrupting Influence, the more SL you must score to avoid gaining Corruption Points.

> **Note:** Spellcasting, or having Spells cast on you, doesn't cause Corruption unless explicitly stated. Simply using a Chaos Lore does not require you or allies to make Corruption Tests.

There are two types of exposure:

- **Exposure:** You have physical contact with the listed source for at least 1 Turn, or spend at least 1 hour in its vicinity or under its influence.
- **Prolonged Exposure:** You have physical contact with the listed source for at least 1 hour, or spend at least 6 hours in its vicinity or under its influence.

#### **Minor Corruption**

These Corrupting Influences, although relatively trivial, still endanger a weak soul. If you fail a Test to resist Minor Corruption, gain 1 Corruption Point.

- **Exposure** to a creature with the *Corruption (Minor)* Trait, refined warpstone, or a profane artefact
- **Prolonged Exposure** to Chaos worshippers, Chaos cult temples, skaven, mutant lairs, and similar

# **Moderate Corruption**

Moderate Corrupting Influences are a danger to every soul and should be avoided under all circumstances. If you fail the Test, gain 2 Corruption Points. On a Marginal Success (+0 SL), gain 1 Corruption Point. On a Success (1+ SL), you gain no Corruption Points.

- **Exposure** to a creature with the *Corruption (Moderate)* Trait or warpstone
- **Prolonged Exposure** to refined warpstone or a Chaos-tainted artefact

#### <span id="page-187-0" data-folio="188"></span>**Major Corruption**

Few mortals can withstand a Major Corrupting Influence for long. Remain in its presence, and Chaos will begin to reshape you. For each failed Major Corruption Test, gain 3 Corruption Points. If you succeed with a Marginal Success (+0 SL), gain 2 Corruption Points. On a Success (1–3 SL), you gain 1 Corruption Point. Only if you score an Impressive Success (4+ SL) do you gain no Corruption Points.

- **Exposure** to a creature with the *Corruption (Major)* Trait
- **Prolonged Exposure** to warpstone
- Making a deal with a daemon
- Consuming refined warpstone

# **Corrupting**

No one is left unscathed by exposure to the Ruinous Powers. Eventually, even the strongest fall, their mind and bodies twisting into new, unrecognisable forms.

Should you ever gain more Corruption Points than your Willpower Bonus plus your Toughness Bonus, immediately attempt a **Challenging (+0 SL) Endurance** Test. If passed, you have managed to hold off your corruption for now but will have to Test again next time you gain Corruption Points. If you fail, then either your body or mind is about to mutate.

# **Dissolution of Body and Mind**

As corruption ravages your soul, the warping breath of Chaos whispers within, either twisting your flesh into a fresh, new form, or fracturing your psyche with exquisite knowledge it can never unlearn.

First, lose Corruption Points equal to your Willpower Bonus as you mutate. Next, roll percentile dice and refer to the following table to determine whether it is your body or your mind that blossoms anew.

Next, roll on either the **Physical Corruption Table** or the **Mental Corruption Table** to see what happens.

### **Manifestation Time**

Mutations seldom manifest immediately. They may begin as an itch, a patch of flaky skin, or a nervous tic, only later developing into a wet and blinking extra eye, unexpected feathers, or a mind twisted beyond recognition. When you generate a mutation, the GM secretly rolls 3d10. The mutation manifests within that many days.

Use the descriptions in the tables to make your mutation uniquely horrifying. If it is obvious to others, you suffer significant penalties to Fellowship Tests as appropriate. More significantly, ordinary people will likely flee from your presence, the watch will be called, you will be pursued by witch hunters, and life will swiftly become very difficult.

# **BE VERY CAREFUL**

Used sparingly, Corruption Points are great fun, but new players may not appreciate how dangerous the Ruinous Powers truly are. Since mutation is effectively a death sentence if discovered, warn players when their actions risk Corruption.

While the player's Character might not be fully aware of Chaos, they should know that tainted artefacts are best left alone, cultists should be avoided at all costs, and mysteriously glowing green rocks are never to be touched. Of course, some adventures leave no choice, but that's **WFRP**.

A corrupted mind may be less obvious than a sprouting tentacle, but it can be just as devastating. Use the description you roll to create a unique mental mutation that suits your Character, developing a believable new mindset. Embrace it as an opportunity to portray your Character in a new, potentially horrifying way.

# **Corruption Limits**

A soul can withstand only so much corruption before it finally gives way. If you ever have more physical mutations than your Toughness Bonus, or more mental mutations than your Willpower Bonus, your soul is lost. Your Character has fallen to Chaos and it is time to create a new one. Your former Character becomes an NPC under the GM's control, and you may yet encounter the wretched creature they have become…
