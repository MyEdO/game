# <span id="page-272-0" data-folio="273"></span>**THE LIE OF THE LAND**

The Reikland is named for the largest river in the Old World, the majestic Reik, which winds through the western Empire and defines the province's eastern and northern borders. The land is blanketed by dense forests, which overshadow the countless settlements, rivers, and roads that cut through the trees.

Much of the land near the Reik is waterlogged and marshy, with long stretches of bogs, swamps, and treacherous mud flats. Away from the wetlands, a multitude of prosperous towns, villages, and cities cling to the riverbanks.

Farther inland, the Reikland climbs upwards to the craggy Skaag Hills and the haunted Hägercrybs, forested uplands hiding isolated regions where one can travel for days without seeing another person. To the west, the forbidding Grey Mountains cast their shadow over the forest-covered lowlands. On the lower slopes, the trees give way to a wide plain of fertile foothills and open grassland known as the Vorbergland.

# **The Mountains, Foothills, and Vorbergland**

The highlands of the Reikland are broken by peaks, crags, and ridges, many topped by ruined castles and watchtowers from bygone eras. Two ranges rise higher than the rest — the Hägercrybs and the Skaag Hills. Further south, the foothills of the Vorbergland lie between the Reikwald forest and the dangerous Grey Mountains.

### **The Grey Mountains**

The forbidding peaks of the Grey Mountains are all but impassable, a near impenetrable border between the Reikland and the Bretonnian duchies. They soar high on the horizon, a constant reminder to Reiklanders of the limits of their land to the west. While there are several dwarf holds here, the Grey Mountains have no true masters and their valleys, plateaus, and slopes harbour all manner of wild creatures, ruins, and mysteries.

Only two reliable passes pierce the frozen heights: the well-defended Axe Bite Pass, guarded by the fortresses of Helmgart and Montfort, and the winding Grey Lady Pass leading from Ubersreik to Parravon. Both passes are guarded by patrols and fortified toll booths. Some impoverished merchants and smugglers take the risk of hiring mountain guides to lead them through lesser passes such as the Crooked Corridor or Durak Way. Many are never seen again.

Countless mines riddle the craggy edges of the Grey Mountains, excavated by order of lords and merchants. Lone watchtowers and stone forts guard these remote settlements against threats from the mountains, many standing amid the rubble of older fortifications that long ago failed in their purpose.

The Skullsmasherz tribe are a mountain-dwelling orc rabble who menace Axe Bite Pass. Under the brutal leadership of Ugrok Craktoof, they range across the mountains from Helmgart to Bretonnia, where Duke d'Montfort is planning to make overtures to Helmgart for an alliance with Margrave von Mackensen against the orcs.

#### <span id="page-273-0" data-folio="274"></span>**DWARF HOLDS OF THE GREY MOUNTAINS**

Many thousands of years ago, the depredations of orcs and goblins forced several clans of dwarfs to abandon their holds in the Worlds Edge Mountains. They journeyed west and eventually settled within the peaks and valleys of the Grey Mountains, hoping to find riches there. The mountains yielded relatively little mineral wealth, but nevertheless, they built holds and began to trade with the primitive humans who dwelled in the foothills.

The Grey Mountain dwarfs are less wealthy than their eastern brethren and their holds are less impressive, yet to human eyes they are marvels of stonework and engineering. While Karak Norn in the south is the preeminent hold in the Grey Mountains, on the borders of the Reikland there are several smaller settlements and two notable holds:

Karak Azgaraz (Hold of the Fearless Axe) is a relatively young hold, situated close to its trading partner, the city of Ubersreik. The hold's king, Thuringar Zaladrinsson, is considered young and intrepid, launching many expeditions against skaven and night goblins in the hold's underdeep.

Karak Ziflin (Windswept Hold) lies to the north of Axe Bite Pass, closer to Bretonnia than the Reikland. It stands within and atop a broad plateau, buffeted by constant winds from the north. Unusually for a dwarf hold, the Upper Deep Halls protrude upwards from the surface of the plateau. King Rorek Granitehand has little love for humans of any kind, but he tolerates Reiklanders as more trustworthy than the Bretonnians to the west.

The dwarfs of the Grey Mountains have not had to suffer the great losses endured by those who remained in the Worlds Edge Mountains, but they are in decline, and no longer have the numbers to maintain the tunnels and outposts they built long ago. Those places are now overrun with orcs and goblins, stone trolls, skaven, and worse. These ancient halls and byways attract the foolhardy and desperate, who delve into the depths in hope of lost dwarf riches.

# **The Hägercrybs**

Dominating the interior of the Reikland, the mist-shrouded Hägercrybs extend from the Princedom of Altdorf in the north to the Duchy of Ubersreik in the south. Dense forest covers the hills and no road penetrates their heights from east to west.

Antiquarians claim the Hägercrybs were the sacred burial ground of the Unberogens, Sigmar's own tribe. Indeed, ancient cairns are found on many of the highlands, some marked by looming menhirs. It is no coincidence that the Hägercrybs have a reputation for being haunted, and only the brave or reckless are willing to stray into its forests. Those who return tell of thick fogs rising from ancient barrows, accompanied by eerie moans echoing from within, supposedly the spirits of ancient kings hungry for the blood of the living.

Ignoring such talk, landowners with holdings in the Hägercrybs order mines sunk into the hills in search of rare metals and minerals. Most fail, their miners never seen again, but a handful succeed and have brought considerable wealth from the earth.

#### **SECRETS OF THE DEFFWEAVE TRIBE**

Hidden amongst the trees on the steepest slopes of the Hägercrybs are the lairs of the Deffweave tribe, a small tribe of extremely vicious goblins. They string up their prey from trees to emulate the spiders they worship. Should a traveller see wild animals and dead miners suspended in the tree canopy above their heads, they *really* ought to turn back.

When the Knights of the Fiery Heart announce a new effort to exterminate the goblins, they beseech anyone able to swing a sword to join them. The wily goblins are not unprepared, and plan to lead any adventurers who encounter them into the lairs of beastmen who also haunt the hills.

# **The Skaag Hills**

The rocky Skaag Hills lie to the west of the River Bögen and run along the southern bank of the Reik before falling beneath the trees of the Duchy of Gorland. In the heart of the range, the forest recedes from the stony crags to reveal layers of stratified rock rising to the scree-laden highlands of the Prie Ridge. A single road crosses the gentler slopes of the Skaag Hills to the south, starting at Trosreut in the shadow of Castle Grauenberg and wending its way through to Holthausen, but many minor tracks and trails also cross the region, leading to abandoned mines and settlements which once produced silver and iron before the seams ran out. Today, outlaws, hunters, and mutants make use of the buildings that remain.

### <span id="page-274-0" data-folio="275"></span>**The Vorbergland**

The Vorbergland is a hilly region extending the length of the Reikland between the Grey Mountains and the edge of the province's forests. It is known for its fertile valleys, rolling grasslands, and windswept plains. The most heavily cultivated regions of the Reikland are the southeastern Vorbergland provinces around Böhrn, Ubersreik, Stimmigen, and Dunkelberg. Flourishing towns, villages, farms, and vineyards pepper the verdant valleys and pastures.

Further west, the Vorbergland is tormented by frequent orc and goblin attacks from the mountains, leaving it little more than a sparsely populated hunting ground for wild animals and monsters from the peaks. This land is popular with game hunters and natural historians from the Imperial Zoo seeking to capture rare creatures, though only the unwise travel this region without a heavy guard and knowledgeable guides.

# **THE CENTREPIECE**

Brandt Brechdt is on the trail of a manticore, or so he claims. He has received a handsome advance from Graf Ostarperht von Haal, who wishes to see the creature killed, stuffed, and displayed as the stunning centrepiece of his vast new dining hall. As time goes by, Brandt is beginning to regret boasting that he knew where to find, and how to kill, such a creature. As the hunt dragged on without sign of the quarry, his underpaid henchmen found more gainful employment. Now Brandt has heard fresh rumours of a manticore hunting in the Vorbergland, and is looking to hire cheap help, such as a band of adventurers, to help him track down the beast and make the kill.

# **Drachenberg**

Soaring high above central Vorbergland, the twisting peak of the Drachenberg is visible for miles around. The mountain has a fell reputation and whenever trouble arises, the nearby townsfolk of Wheburg cast an eye towards the mountain and make the comet sign of Sigmar to ward off evil.

The mountain has long been a favoured haunt of the dragons, as well as basilisks, wyverns, and manticores. The immense red dragon, Caledair, the 'Scythe of Fire', once made her lair in caves near the peak of the mountain, scouring the Vorbergland when rage or hunger brought her forth. She has not been seen for more than a century, though none can say for certain if she has migrated to parts unknown or sleeps within the mountain.

Rumours of dragons' hoards draw treasure hunters to the Drachenberg, but most are thwarted by the mountain's treacherous slopes long before they ever encounter a dragon.

# **The Grim, Dark Forests**

The widespread forests of the Reikland do not have the terrifying reputation of the Drakwald or the Forest of Shadows, but away from the busy rivers and settlements, uncharted miles of towering oak, pine, elm, and beech hide much more than desperate bandits and wild animals.

# **The Reikwald**

Other than the Vorbergland, the Reikwald blankets almost the entire Reikland, broken only by rivers, roads, and scattered settlements. The waterways offer safer passage than the uncertain roads, which wind through the favoured haunts of outcasts, cut-throats, and mutants. The roadwardens cannot patrol in sufficient force to keep the byways safe, so upturned coaches and other signs of banditry are a common sight.

Outlaw bands claim open clearings and abandoned ruins, where they lie in wait to ambush the unwary or attack farms and villages. The deepest woods harbour sizeable beastman warherds, who could pour forth from the trees at any time to raid, despoil, and murder. Regiments of the Reikland State Army make occasional forays to clear these isolated places of the beasts within, though it is impossible to penetrate every corner of the forest.

The deepest woods are shadowed and gloomy. Heavy fogs are common, especially along the marshier stretches of the Reik. Scholars claim that the most ancient human tribes ranged across this misty forest. Concentric rings of carved standing stones called Ogham stones remain from a time long before Sigmar, though many are overgrown and almost impossible to find without a guide. Magisters from the Colleges of Magic postulate terrible battles were once fought for these ancient, megalithic sites, and it is not uncommon to find wizards funding explorations into the Reikwald's depths to learn more about the magical stones and their properties.

# **Bloodpine Woods**

The wood of the bloodpine tree is a deep maroon colour and prized by artisans for crafting exquisite furniture, which commands high prices in Altdorf, Marienburg, and Nuln. The Bloodpine Woods are an outcrop of these trees growing down the southern slopes of the Amber Hills. The lumber is difficult to source due to the Spiderclaw tribe, goblins who ride on swift-moving giant spiders, led by an ambitious shaman, Raknik Spiderclaw. Many a woodcutter has met a grisly fate as food for the monstrous arachnids and their masters. As bloodpine prices increase, merchants are willing to pay mercenaries and others to protect their supply of valuable timber.

# <span id="page-275-0" data-folio="276"></span>**THE GROVE OF RHULITHRI**

Where the Reikwald covers the Amber Hills, the elves of the Laurelorn forest have established a permanent camp by order of Queen Marrisith. Amongst a grove of ash and oak, they have trained the boughs of trees to form halls and lodges for any of their people who find themselves in the Reikland feeling wary or weary of human company.

Dathior Brightshade established the settlement several generations ago and now oversees Rhulithri on behalf of her queen. The proximity to Altdorf and the emperor's throne is intentional, most of the elves are wise enough to realise that the fate of the Laurelorn is tied to that of the human realm.

While outsiders are not welcome, humans and elves from Ulthuan and Athel Loren are tolerated if they show due respect to their hosts. In recent times, a high elf noble, Princess Kaele of Tor Dynal, has been visiting the vale. Queen Marrisith's subjects consider her cold and distant, but the Cothiqui princess insists that her hosts allow her to see the encampment.

#### **The Grissenwald**

The trees of the Grissenwald grow close together, with distorted trunks and twisted undergrowth. In the darkest woods skulk beastmen, witches, and tribes of feral mutants, who prowl the borders for victims. Most of the local woodsmen travel in groups and seldom stay outdoors come nightfall, and it is commonplace to find fluttering bills posted on roadside trees, offering rewards for the retrieval of family members and friends thought to be lost in the bowels of the forest.

Deep in the interior is a ring of towering monoliths known as the High Circle, where woodsmen believe witches and hedgefolk make pacts with the beastmen.

# **KINBANDS OF TOR SARATHLUI**

Asrai are rare outside Athel Loren and shun the company of other species. However, some asrai are 'lost kindreds', wood elves who settled outside the Loren Forest and maintain their hinterglades in other lands. The Reikwald has one notable asrai settlement, Tor Sarathlui.

Spellsingers have wielded cunning magics to conceal these glades from all but the wood elves. The kinbands here number no more than 150 elves, dwelling around a wooden tower overgrown with vines. This emerges from a stand of wythel trees, planted here many generations ago.

# **The Rivers, Canals, and Lakes**

The Reikland's wealth and economy is largely dependent on the many rivers spilling from glacial lakes and waterfalls in the Grey Mountains, which run through the province to join the mighty Reik. Barges brimful with merchandise navigate the largest of these rivers, shipping goods throughout the Empire or downstream to the great markets of Marienburg.

The richest noble and merchant houses have invested in extensive canals to allow goods move faster than before. The free flow of trade is always under threat, however, from wreckers and river pirates. The roadwardens, guards, and riverwardens are too overstretched to protect every barge, and a good many are no better than the criminals they supposedly pursue.

#### **GRANDFATHER REIK**

Grandfather Reik is a minor god, the patron of the river itself. He is usually depicted as a naked, elderly man with an immensely long beard, entangled with eels and other fish. Also known as the Reikgott, he has no defined cult and no dedicated worshippers as such. Rather, all who depend on the Reik in some way pay respect to Grandfather Reik. They offer small sacrifices and prayers of gratitude at his shrines on islands and the banks of the river.

The priests of Manann, the jealous sea god, maintain that Grandfather Reik is either an aspect of Manann or one of his divine children. Yet many in the Reikland will never see the sea and prefer their own river god to a distant briny deity.

# **The Reik**

The Reik is the mightiest river in the Old World, rising in the Black Mountains before winding through the Empire to meet the Sea of Claws at Marienburg. The many tributaries which flow into the Reik drain the Reikland and much of the western Empire beyond. It is a vital river for the land's prosperity; the waters of the Reik carry more traffic than the rest of the Empire's rivers combined.

North of Nuln, the Reik carves a broad valley through the Reikland, with increasing numbers of riverside settlements as it flows towards the Wasteland. Its span is so broad that it cannot be bridged downstream from Nuln until it reaches Altdorf, where it joins the Talabec and divides into a complex tracework of channels. These spread to form the Altdorf Flats, as the main channel flows through Altdorf, still wide, but narrow enough for the city's five Reik bridges. The capital's preeminence as a trade centre is due, in part, to the fact it is the only place for hundreds of miles where land traffic can cross the Reik.

<span id="page-276-0" data-folio="277"></span>Downstream of Altdorf, the channels reconverge to form the deepest stretch of the Reik, broadening until the opposite bank disappears into the mists drifting across the water. Ocean-going vessels sail these waters, including the First Fleet of the Imperial Navy, whose warships travel to and from the deepwater harbour at Altdorf 's Reiksport.

The westernmost Reik is replete with rocky islands. Some are crowned with ancient fortresses, now overrun by river pirates, while others have been abandoned entirely, home to little more than a handful of old smugglers' coves.

# **THE LORLAY**

The Lorlay is an imposing rock formation rising from the middle of the Reik, some 40 miles downstream of Grissenwald. The channels on either side of the jutting island run swift and deep, posing a hazard to boatmen unfamiliar with their treacherous cross-currents. Romantic legends tell of a beautiful maiden, pale of skin and supple of limb, who swims these waters at dawn and dusk. Tales of the singing water maiden are especially common among the Reikland's sailors, and if they are to be believed, half claim to have seen her frolicking in the river**.**

# **The Bögen**

The people of Bögenhafen thank Bögenauer, their river's patron deity, for their prosperity. The river's clear, placid waters flow gently enough to allow trade barges to sail upriver nearly as easily as the current takes them downstream.

The Bögen's source lies high in the freezing peaks of the Grey Mountains, warming as it flows through the Vorbergland hills. In the evenings, tendrils of roiling fog rise from its waters, wrapping about nearby settlements, penetrating the Reikwald and obscuring the deeds of those who would rather stay hidden.

#### **The Teufel**

The Teufel flows down from the mountains to Ubersreik, then northwards through Auerswald and Grünburg to the Reik. Iron deposits in the silt lend it a ruddy hue, though storytellers say this comes from the unending wars between the Grey Mountain dwarfs and goblins, which permanently stain the waters blood red. The Teufel is prone to flooding, especially in spring, when its waters inundate the river's forested banks.

# **The Canals**

Over several centuries labourers have excavated canals to link the Reik and its many tributaries, making trade and travel easier and safer for those who use the waterways.

**The Grünberg Canal** — commissioned by Emperor Luitpold III and completed in 2506 IC, this canal bypasses the treacherous shallows of the Reiker Marshes and carries most of the Teufel's barge traffic to Altdorf. A tollhouse at Grünburg extracts a river tax based on vessel length. At the north end of the canal is a lock keeper's house on the outskirts of Prieze. This doubles as a barracks and stables for the twelve roadwardens who patrol the canal's towpath.

**The Vorbergland Canals** — five canals which carry trade from Wissenland across the Vorbergland and beyond. The canal system links Nuln to Carroburg, avoiding Altdorf 's high river taxes. The dwarfs of Karak Azgaraz have recently demanded the canals close, claiming the former archduke of Upper Teufel was violating several treaties by commissioning such poor workmanship from Altdorf 's Imperial School of Engineers. The engineers are deeply offended, as their steam locks and clever water pumps are state of the art for humans.

**The Weissbruck Canal** — this canal carries steady traffic to and from Altdorf, connecting the Bögen and the Reik, while bypassing Carroburg's excisemen. Local bargees whisper strange stories about the canal. While excavating the waterway, dwarf engineers supposedly unearthed prehistoric artefacts which predated even the Grey Mountain dwarf holds. Things have never been right in the area since and some swear that when the moon Morrsleib is full, one can sail the canal north to somewhere other than the familiar waters of the Reik.

# **The Cursed and Fetid Marshlands**

Although Middenland claims many famous marshlands, such as the Furdienst, Midden Marshes, and Shadensumpf, the river banks of the Reikland are no less waterlogged, commonly giving way to fens, mires, and dangerous mud plains.

#### **Grootscher Marsh**

The largest wetland in the Reikland, the Grootscher Marsh spreads from both banks of the Reik in the west of the province. It extends some fifty miles into the Reikland and Middenland from the border with the Wasteland. Many consider it a cursed place, for it is the site of one of the Empire's most famous military defeats of the last century: the Battle of Grootscher Marsh, where the armies of the merchant princes of the Wasteland secured independence from Imperial rule.

<span id="page-277-0" data-folio="278"></span>The foul-smelling fenland is populated by flocks of ill-sounding birds and infested with river trolls. In lean years when food is scarce, the trolls grow hungry and slip into the Reik to pluck the unwary from passing ships. Periodically, regiments of the Reikland State Army are sent to clear out the Grootscher Marsh, one of the worst punishment details imaginable, and even the hardiest soldiers fear for their lives.

Travellers on the Reik occasionally report the sound of strange, ominous horns echoing through thick fog that gathers swiftly across the marsh. Stories of ancient swamp daemons may be more than mere legend, for riverfolk claim shadowy, shambling figures can be glimpsed moving through the mist.

# **SHROOM BOOM**

Physicians, apothecaries, and scholars all pay high prices for the multitude of rare mushrooms and unusual plants that grow in the perilous Cursed Marsh about the city of Marienburg. In recent times, spores of daemon's tand, rood puffball, and dodeshors polypore have drifted east and sprouted in the murk of the Grootscher Marsh. These fungi are especially valuable and, sensing heavy profits, the merchant Klaes Adaans is hiring mushroom-pickers to set out from Oberseert to forage on his behalf. Fungi hunters have attracted hungry river trolls, as well as bands of swamp-dwelling goblins, hoping to capture the trolls and gorge themselves on mushrooms. Klaes keeps this information to himself; he just wants a steady crop of lucrative fungus. Those with stern hearts and keen eyes can reap real rewards here, or become meat for a troll's belly.

#### **The Altdorf Flats**

The rivers Reik and Talabec split into hundreds of shifting channels, black-soiled mudflats, and marshes that spread north and south of Altdorf. These wetlands are choked with rushes and reeds, with several causeways winding outwards from the city, each one busy with coaches, merchant trains, and heavily patrolled by roadwardens. Stone bridges carry these roads over ditches and mires, including fine dwarf constructions dating back to the time of Sigmar.

Within the Altdorf Flats is a marsh known as the Brackenwalsch, a place of mystery for the Unberogen tribe which called the Reikland home many thousands of years ago. Their kings sought counsel with the Thrice-Cursed Crone of the Brackenwalsch, an ancient crone who gave them cryptic portents of the future.

#### **Reiker Marshes**

Between the towns of Prieze and Babenborn on the Reik, the sluggish confluence with the Teufel spreads across the low-lying land in a myriad of reedy channels. The Reiker Marshes are notoriously treacherous, and inexperienced captains frequently run aground in their deceptively shallow waters. Tattered flags and corroded signposts mark the most perilous sections, but these are woefully insufficient. River guides known as huffers are available to guide boats through the waters for a reasonable price, but wise barge captains avoid the marshes completely, preferring to pay tolls to pass through the Grünberg Canal rather than risk their crew and cargo.

# **THE FOULHERD**

Deep in the Reiker Marshes, a few miles north of the Reik, stands a rotting copse of dead trees. These maggot-ridden trunks once grew from a hillock part-submerged in the mire, and now play host to the Foulherd, a warband of beastmen in thrall to the Chaos God Nurgle. The braylord is a beastman champion of Chaos known as Bilehoof, who skulks amongst his pestigor brethren spreading infestations of parasites and imploring them to give up their flesh to the Plague Lord. Bilehoof seeks to build a towering pile of carcasses amongst the dead trees, so that they will rot in the fetid air and incubate daemonic maggots gifted to him by Nurgle.

# **Uhland Bogs**

The Westerfluss river is the natural border between the Wasteland and the Reikland, which cuts through a windswept peatland known as the Uhland Bogs. Villagers living nearby venture into the mossy bogs to cut and dry peat, which they sell along the Reik for fuel. In the southwest depths of the bog, a number of ancient, carved stones rise from the sodden land, drawing local cultists of Rhya and older deities to worship during important equinoxes.

# **THE CROWSTONES**

At the heart of a black bog is a circle called the Crowstones, which have a particularly foul reputation. Locals warn never to travel to the stones near the festival days of Geheimnistag or Hexenstag, for they claim crows gather in impossible numbers and unspeakable things rise from the bog to wreak terror upon the living.
