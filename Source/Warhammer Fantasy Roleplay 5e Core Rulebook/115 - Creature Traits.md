# <span id="page-355-0" data-folio="356"></span>**CREATURE TRAITS**

### **Afraid (Target)**

The creature gains Fear of the Target. When encountered, they must make an Extended Psychology Test (page 183). Until their SL equals or exceeds the Target's Fear Rating, the creature cannot gain Momentum, and Melee Attacks made against them by the Target benefit from Advantage. See page 183 for *Fear* rules.

### **Amphibious**

The creature is as at home in water as they are on land. They have the Swim Skill, add +1 SL to all Swim Tests, and move at full Movement through water.

### **Animosity (Target)**

The creature dislikes the Target. When the creature encounters the object of its Animosity, make a Psychology Test (page 183). On a failure, it must attack the target socially or physically, as appropriate, gaining +1 SL on Fellowship based Tests to oppose or disparage them. See page 183 for full *Animosity* rules.

### **Belligerent**

The creature loves to win a fight. As long as they possess Momentum, they are immune to *Fear*.

### **Bestial**

The creature has no rational thought or language. It shies away from fire and gains a *Broken* Condition if struck and wounded by it. In defence, it only uses the *Dodge* Skill. If it loses more than half its Wounds, it will attempt to Flee unless protecting its young or cornered, or unless it has the *Territorial* Trait. If so, it enters *Frenzy* (see page 184).

#### **Bite**

The creature may lose Momentum to make a Free Attack. The Damage equals Rating and includes their Strength Bonus already.

**Free Attack** - Weapon Skill or Ballistic Skill attack that does not use your Action on your Turn.

## **Blessed (Deity)**

The creature can enact Blessings, the relevant deity is indicated in parentheses. A creature with this Trait has a Pray Skill at Fel + 10 (or more at the GM's discretion).

## <span id="page-356-0" data-folio="357"></span>**Bounce**

The creature can bounce high, perhaps with powerful limbs, magic, or stubby wings. When Charging, Leaping, or Sprinting, they double their Movement Attribute and can ignore all intervening terrain and creatures as they leap over them.

### **Breath**

The creature's breath is a powerful weapon. As a ranged attack, they can activate their *Breath* as a Free Attack, targeting 1 creature they can see within 20 + Toughness Bonus yards and all creatures within SB yards of that target. The attacker makes an Opposed Ballistic Skill/Dodge Test against all targets (the attacker's single roll Opposed by each individual target). If a creature without a BS makes a Breath Attack, count it as having a BS of 30. Losing targets take Rating Damage. Further, if the Trait has any of the following Types in parentheses, apply the associated rules.

- **Acid:** Inflicts Damage of TB +4. All weapons and armour carried by the targets suffer 1 Damage.
- **Cold:** Inflicts Damage of SB +2. Targets gain a *Fatigued* Condition; until they lose the Condition, their weapons gain the *Slow* Flaw.
- **Electricity:** Inflicts Damage of SB +2. Damage ignores metal armour. Targets gain a *Stunned* Condition.
- **Fire:** Inflicts Damage of SB +3. Targets gain an *Ablaze* Condition.
- **Poison:** Inflicts Damage of TB +2. Targets gain a *Poisoned* Condition.
- **Smoke:** The area fills with smoke, blocking line of sight and causing Suffocation (page 169) for Toughness Bonus Rounds.

The creature is immune to their own *Breath*. This attack is *Magical*.

## **Champion**

The creature is an extraordinarily skilled warrior. If they win an Opposed Test when defending in melee combat, they can cause Damage just as if they were the attacker.

### **Chill Grasp**

The creature's touch chills their enemies' souls. If they lose Momentum, then for their Action, they can attempt an Opposed Weapon Skill/Dodge or Melee Test. If they win, the target loses 1d10 + SL Wounds ignoring Toughness Bonus and Armour Points. This attack is *Magical*.

### **Cold-blooded**

The creature is cold-blooded and slow to react. They gain Advantage to Cool Tests. If they gain the *Surprised* Condition, this Condition is not lost the first time it should be (typically at the end of the Round or if they are attacked).

### **Constrictor**

The creature can squeeze and crush their prey. Any successful hit gives the target an *Entangled* Condition. The creature may then enter a Grapple if they wish (see page 167).

### **Construct**

The creature is a construct of magic, quite mindless, and bound together with enchantments. It has no Intelligence, Willpower, or Fellowship Characteristics, and need never Test them. If it is ever called upon to Test based on Willpower, use Toughness instead. If it has no spellcaster controlling it, or does not possess the *Territorial* Trait, it wanders mindlessly. For the purpose of calculating its Wounds, use its Strength Bonus whenever Willpower Bonus is required. All its attacks are *Magical*.

### **Corrosive Blood**

The creature's blood is corrosive. Every time they are wounded, blood splashes free and all targets Engaged with them take 1d10 Damage (roll once and apply to all targets).

### **Corruption (Strength)**

The creature is tainted by Chaos, or perhaps suffused with Dark Magics. They are a Corrupting Influence with a Strength marked in parentheses. See page 187.

#### **Dark Vision**

The creature can see in the dark as if it were daylight.

### **Daemonic (Rating)**

The creature's essence is raw magic, and unholy ichor pumps through what passes for their veins. Daemonic creatures do not require the normal prerequisites for life such as food, water, air, and so on.

All their attacks are *Magical*. Roll 1d10 after any blow is received, if the creature rolls Rating or higher, the blow is ignored, even if it is a Critical. They may not use this ability to ignore *Magical*  blows such as those caused by creatures with the *Magical* Trait or weapons with the *Magical* Quality. Should the creature be reduced to 0 Wounds, their soul returns to the Realms of Chaos immediately, removing them from play.

### <span id="page-357-0" data-folio="358"></span>**Disease (Type)**

The creature carries the disease listed, though whether they suffer from the disease is up to the GM. Others will have to Test as appropriate for Contraction (see page 179).

### **Distracting**

The creature distracts or confuses foes, possibly exuding a soporific musk or nauseating reek, or through its bizarre and horrifying appearance. All living targets within a number of yards equal to the creature's Toughness Bonus suffer a -2 SL penalty to all Tests. A target can only suffer this penalty once, no matter how many *Distracting* foes there are.

### **Ethereal**

The creature's form is insubstantial, allowing them to pass through solid objects. They can only be harmed by *Magical*  attacks and all their attacks are *Magical*.

# **Fear (Rating)**

The creature causes supernatural *Fear* in other creatures. Opponents must make an Extended Psychology Test (page 183). Until they have accumulated more SL than the creature's *Fear* Rating (1, if not stated), they cannot gain Momentum, and melee attacks against them benefit from Advantage. See page 183 for *Fear* rules.

### **Fly (Rating)**

As the creature's Move, they can fly up to Rating yards. When flying, they ignore all intervening terrain, obstacles, or creatures. At the end of the move, they decide whether they have landed or are still flying. They can use this move to Charge. If they start their turn flying, they must choose to Fly for their Move. If they cannot do this, the GM decides how far the creature falls (see page 169).

When targeting them, measure horizontal distance as normal, then increase range by 1 step. So, a Long Range shot would become Extreme Range, and if they were at Extreme Range, they could not be shot at all. When flying, they suffer a -20 penalty to Ranged Attacks as they swoop and wheel in the sky.

### **Frenzy**

The creature may make a Willpower Test to enter a *Frenzy*  (page 184). While *Frenzied*, they are immune to Psychology, cannot flee or retreat, must attack the nearest visible enemy, gain a Free Attack each Round, +1 Damage, and may only attack or move to attack. *Frenzy* ends when no enemies remain in sight, or if the creature becomes *Stunned* or *Unconscious*. Afterwards, they gain a *Fatigued* Condition.

### **Ghostly Howl**

The creature can emit a hideous scream, capable of killing those who hear it. Once per turn, this creature can lose their Momentum to unleash a howl as a Free Attack. All living targets within a number of yards equal to the creature's Strength who can hear it immediately gain 3 *Deafened* Conditions and suffer 1d10 Wounds ignoring Toughness Bonus and Armour Points. Those affected must also pass an **Average (+2 SL) Cool** Test or gain a *Broken* Condition. This attack is *Magical*.

### **Grim**

The creature is incredibly lethal, able to quickly gain the upper hand over almost any opponent. At the end of its turn, if the creature does not have Momentum (page 167), it gains it.

### **Hatred (Target)**

When the creature encounters the Target, they must make a Psychology Test (page 183). On a failure, they become subject to *Hatred*. While *Hatred* lasts, they must attack the hated foe by the fastest and most deadly means possible, gain +1 SL on Melee and Ranged Tests against them, and are immune to *Intimidate* and *Fear* (but not *Terror*) caused by the Target. See page 184 for full *Hatred* rules.

### **Horns**

The creature has horns or some other sharp appendage (if their *Horns* Trait represents a different feature, it will be noted in parentheses). When the creature charges into combat, they may make a Free Attack with their *Horns*, with a Damage of SB +4.

### **Hungry**

The creature is always hungry for fresh meat. If they kill or incapacitate a living opponent (or encounter a fresh body), they must pass an **Average (+2 SL) Cool** Test or feast, losing their next Move and Action.

### **Immune to Psychology**

Whether brave, stupid, or just caught up in the moment, the creature is utterly stoic. They ignore the Psychology rules (see page 183).

### **Immunity (Type)**

The creature is completely immune to a certain type of harm, such as poison, magic, or electricity. All harm of that type, including from a Critical Wound, is ignored.

### <span id="page-358-0" data-folio="359"></span>**Infected**

The creature, or their weapon, carries a nasty infection. If they cause a living opponent to lose Wounds, the opponent must pass an **Easy (+4 SL) Endurance** Test or contract a Festering Wound (see page 180).

### **Infestation**

The creature's hide is infested with biting fleas or similar. All opponents suffer a penalty of -1 SL to hit them in melee combat as the parasites distract and overwhelm them.

### **Many Heads (Number)**

The creature has more than one head. If a Critical Wound affecting the head stuns, kills, or renders the creature unconscious it will only affect the creature once all the heads are either disabled or affected to a similar degree. If the creature has the *Bite*, *Horns*, or *Tongue* Traits it can make a number of Free Attacks equal to the number of healthy heads. The creature benefits from +2 SL to Perception Tests whilst it has at least two healthy heads.

# **Mark of Chaos (God)**

One of the dark powers has marked this creature with a physical brand, proclaiming it one of their favoured servants. The exact effects of this mark depend on which of the Chaos gods bestowed the Mark.

**Khorne:** The creature benefits from the Frenzy Talent. This creature gains the *Etiquette* (*Followers of Khorne*) Talent and is subject to *Animosity* towards overt followers of Slaanesh. The creature is also subject to *Animosity* by followers of Slaanesh, so long as the Mark of Khorne is visible. The Character may not use the Language (Magick) or Channelling skills unless it is to dispel a spell. Additionally, they may purchase the following Talents as if they were Career Advances for the normal XP costs: *Berserk Charge, Combat Aware, Combat Reflexes, Furious Assault, Implacable, Magic Resistance, Resistance (Magic), Resolute, Strike Mighty Blow, Warrior Born*.

**Nurgle:** The creature benefits from a bonus of +10 to its Toughness. This creature gains the *Etiquette* (*Followers of Nurgle*) Talent and is subject to *Animosity* towards overt followers of Tzeentch. The creature is also subject to *Animosity* by followers of Tzeentch, so long as the Mark of Nurgle is visible. Additionally they may purchase the following Talents as if they were Career Advances for the normal XP costs: *Frightening, Hardy, Implacable, Iron Jaw, Menacing, Resistance (Poison), Resistance (Disease), Robust, Tenacious*, and *Very Resilient*.

> **Slaanesh:** The creature gains the *Fearless* (*Everything*) Talent. This creature gains the *Etiquette* (*Followers of Slaanesh*) Talent and is subject to *Animosity* towards overt followers of Khorne. The creature is also subject to *Animosity* by followers of Khorne, so long as the Mark of Slaanesh is visible. Additionally, they may purchase the following Talents as if they were Career Advances for the normal XP costs: *Attractive, Blather, Careful Strike, Combat Master, Gregarious, Inspiring, Lightning Reflexes, Nimblefingered, Resistant (Poison)*, and *Sharp*.

> **Tzeentch:** This creature gains 1d10/3 Mutations (rounding up), alternating between Mental and Physical Mutations. This creature gains *Etiquette* (*Followers of Tzeentch*), and is subject to *Animosity* towards overt followers of Nurgle. The creature is also subject to *Animosity* by followers of Nurgle, so long as the Mark of Tzeentch can be seen. Additionally, this creature may purchase the following Talents as if they were Career Advances: *Aethyric Attunement, Arcane Magic (Any), Chaos Magic (Tzeentch), Fast Hands, Instinctive Diction, Magical Sense, Petty Magic, Second Sight, War Wizard*, and *Witch!* for the normal XP costs.

### **Magical**

The creature is wreathed in magic. Their attacks count as Magical, and they can harm creatures only susceptible to *Magical* attacks.

### **Magic Resistance**

The creature is resistant to magic. Any Casting Test to cast a Spell that would affect them suffers -3 SL. No creature with this Trait may possess the Channelling Skill or the *Arcane Magic, Bless, Chaos Magic, Invoke, Petty Magic,* or *Witch!* Talents, and vice versa.

### **Mental Corruption**

The creature has Chaos on the mind. Roll on the **Mental Corruption Table** found on page 189.

# **Miracles (Deity)**

The creature can enact Miracles; the relevant deity is indicated in parentheses. A creature with this Trait has a Pray Skill at Fel + 10 (or more at the GM's discretion).

### <span id="page-359-0" data-folio="360"></span>**Mutation**

The creature bears a Mutation. Roll on the **Physical Corruption Table** found on page 189.

## **Night Vision**

The creature can see very well in natural darkness. They can see clearly in such darkness for 20 yards and can extend the effective illumination distance of any light sources by a further 20 yards.

### **Painless**

The creature is impervious to pain. All non-amputation penalties suffered from Critical Wounds are ignored, although Conditions are suffered as normal.

### **Petrifying Gaze**

The creature's gaze can turn flesh to stone. As an Action, the creature may lose its Momentum to unleash its petrifying gaze against one living target it can see. The creature makes a Ballistic Skill Test, opposed by the target's Initiative. If the creature wins, it inflicts a *Stunned* Condition, and one more for each SL after the first. If they win by 6+ SL, the target is permanently turned to stone. If the target is a spellcaster, they can oppose the Test with *Language (Magick)* instead of *Initiative*.

### **Regeneration**

The creature can heal at an extraordinary rate, even regrowing severed parts. At the start of each Round, if they have more than 0 Wounds remaining, they automatically regenerate 1d10 Wounds. If they have 0 Wounds remaining, they regenerate a single Wound on a 1d10 roll of 8+. If they ever roll a 10 for regenerating, they also fully regenerate a Critical Wound, losing all penalties and Conditions associated with it. Any Wounds or Critical Wounds caused by fire may not be regenerated.

### **Size (Various)**

This Trait represents creatures whose size differs from the game standard (i.e. roughly human-sized). There are five steps of Size, ranging from Tiny to Monstrous.

| Size      | Examples                             |
|-----------|--------------------------------------|
| Tiny      | Butterfly, Mouse, Pigeon, Cat, Hawk, |
|           | Human Infant                         |
| Average   | Giant Rat, Halfling, Human Child,    |
|           | Dwarf, Elf, Human                    |
| Large     | Horse, Ogre, Troll                   |
| Enormous  | Griffon, Wyvern, Manticore           |
| Monstrous | Dragon, Giant, Greater Daemon        |

#### **Size Combat Modifiers**

Large creatures do more Damage when they land a hit. These modifiers don't apply to Ranged Attacks or extra attacks. NPC profiles already include these modifiers.

- **Small:** Damage as normal
- **Average:** Damage as normal
- **Large:** Damage as normal plus additional SB Damage
- **Enormous:** Damage as normal plus additional SB Damage
- **Monstrous:** Damage as normal plus additional (2 x SB) Damage

#### **Defending Against Big Creatures**

When fighting a creature of larger size, you suffer a penalty of -2 SL when using *Melee* to defend in an Opposed Test. It is better to dodge a giant swinging a tree trunk than try to parry it!

#### **Fear**

Agressive creatures cause *Fear* in those smaller than them.

#### **Moving in Combat**

Creatures ignore the need to *Disengage* from melee combat with smaller combatants.

#### **Opposed Strength**

During Opposed Strength Tests (and similar), if one creature is 2 or more *Size* steps larger, they win automatically. If one creature is 1 *Size* step larger, the smaller creature must score a Critical to contest the roll. If they do, SL are compared as normal. All other results mean the larger creature wins.

#### **Outnumbering Larger Creatures**

Larger creatures count as several creatures for the sake of working out if they are outnumbered in combat.

- **Small:** Counts as 1 creature
- **Average:** Counts as 1 creature
- **Large:** Counts as 2 creatures
- **Enormous:** Counts as 3 creatures
- **Monstrous:** Counts as 4 creatures

So, you would need 4 *Size (Average)* creatures to outnumber a *Size (Large)* creature by 2 to 1, or 3 *Size (Large)* creatures to outnumber a *Size (Monstrous)* creature by 2 to 1.

Conversely, for the sake of larger opponents being daunting enough, they do not themselves count as multiple opponents when working out if they receive a bonus for outnumbering themselves. 2 *Size (Large)* Ogres outnumber 1 *Size (Average)* Human 2 to 1, not 4 to 1.

#### <span id="page-360-0" data-folio="361"></span>**Stomp**

Creatures that are larger than their opponents may lose Momentum to make one Stomp as a Free Attack, kicking downwards or otherwise bashing smaller opponents out of the way. This attack has a Damage equal to their Strength Bonus and uses *Melee (Brawling)*.

# **Wounds**

Larger creatures have more Wounds:

| Size      | Wounds                                                            |
|-----------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Small     | 2 × Toughness Bonus                                               |
| Average   | Strength Bonus + (2 × Toughness Bonus) + Willpower Bonus       |
| Large     | (Strength Bonus + (2 × Toughness Bonus) + Willpower Bonus) × 2 |
| Enormous  | (Strength Bonus + (2 × Toughness Bonus) + Willpower Bonus) × 4 |
| Monstrous | (Strength Bonus + (2 × Toughness Bonus) + Willpower Bonus) × 8 |

### **Skittish**

The creature is scared by spectacular visible effects of magic or noises such as thunderclaps or explosions. If such occurs, they receive +3 *Broken* Conditions.

### **Spellcaster (Various)**

The creature can cast spells; the specific Lore(s) of Magic are indicated in parentheses. Add the Spellcaster or Spellcaster Lord template or, if that wouldn't be appropriate, add Channelling (Choose a Wind) (WP +10) and Language (Magick) (Int + 10) as if they were Basic Skills. The GM may increase the numbers of skills if they deem it appropriate.

### **Sprinter**

The creature has a long stride, perhaps because it is a quadruped or has especially long legs. Multiply Run Movement by 1.5 when Running.

## **Stealthy**

The creature is especially stealthy. They are granted the Stealth (Rural) Skill at Ag +10. This number may be increased at the GM's discretion.

### **Striding Gait (Terrain)**

The creature is at home in the listed terrain. They suffer no penalties for moving through the listed terrain and add +1 SL to Athletics and Stealth Tests in the listed terrain.

# **ADJUSTING A CREATURE'S SIZE**

If you wish to use Size to make a creature bigger then increase *Strength* and *Toughness* by +10 and reduce *Agility* by -5 per step of size you increase the creature. Reverse this if you wish to make a creature smaller.

**Example:** *The Giant Spider described in the Bestiary is Size (Small) with a Strength of 15, Toughness of 25, and an Agility of 35. If it were made Size (Large) it would have a Strength of 35, Toughness of 45, and Agility of 25.*

*Its Wounds would increase to 26, its fangs would inflict +5 Damage and its Bite would increase to +6 Damage.*

# <span id="page-361-0" data-folio="362"></span>**Stupid**

While not devoid of self-awareness (and so lacking the *Bestial* Trait), the creature is stupid. If they are near any allies without the *Stupid* Trait, they guide them and nothing happens. Otherwise, they must pass an **Easy (+4 SL) Intelligence** Test at the start of each Round or become very confused. Should this occur, they will drool, perhaps sitting down or picking their nose, doing little of use, losing both their Move and Action for that Turn.

# **Swarm**

Swarms are large numbers of the same creature acting as one. The swarm counts as a single creature that ignores the Psychology rules (see page 183), and can ignore the Engaged rules when using its Move. All opponents Engaged with a Swarm automatically lose 1 Wound at the end of every Round as the Swarm overwhelms anything close. The Swarm has five times the Wounds of a normal example of the creature and gains +10 Weapon Skill. Any attempts to shoot the Swarm gain a +40 bonus to hit. Swarms ignore all the *Size*  Creature Trait rules.

# **Tail**

The creature's tail is capable of sweeping foes from their feet. They may lose Momentum to make a Free Attack that does SB +2 Damage. Smaller-*Size* targets that suffer any Wounds from the attack also gain the *Prone* Condition.

### **# Tentacles**

The creature has a number of tentacles equal to #. They gain one Free Attack per tentacle. Each tentacle's attack does Damage of SB +0. If they cause Damage, they can also give their opponent an *Entangled* Condition, which will initiate a Grapple between the target and that tentacle. If a tentacle is Grappling, use the tentacle's Free Attack to resolve that Grapple, not the creature's Action (see page 167).

### **Territorial**

This creature is protective of a particular area or location. They will fight to the death to protect it and will not normally pursue enemies if they flee this area.

# **Terror (Rating)**

The creature supernaturally causes bone-chilling *Terror*. When first encountering the creature, opponents must make a Psychology Test (page 183). On a failure, opponents gain *Broken* Conditions equal to the *Terror* Rating, plus 1 for each negative SL. Regardless of the result, it also causes *Fear* (page 183) with an equal *Fear* Rating.

# **Tongue**

The creature's prehensile tongue can wrap itself around prey, dragging it to a grisly end. They may lose Momentum to make a Free Attack. This is a Ranged Attack that does Damage of SB +0. It is up to the GM to decide on the range, though a default is 3 yards for creatures of *Size (Small to Large)* and 12 yards for larger creatures. If the attack hits, the target receives 1 *Entangled* Condition and, if a smaller *Size*, is dragged towards the creature, and is Engaged in melee combat. The creature can then choose whether to release the target, perform a Free Attack using its *Weapon* Trait, or keep the target wrapped in their tongue, initiating a Grapple (see page 167).

# <span id="page-362-0" data-folio="363"></span>**Tracker**

Trackers are adept at following their prey, generally through scent or hearing. The creature is granted the Track Skill at Int +10. This number may be increased at the GM's discretion.

# **Trained (Trained Skills)**

This Trait represents animals that have been trained through the *Animal Training* Skill. The skills the animal knows are marked in the parentheses. Feel free to create your own *Trained* skills.

**Broken:** The animal is trained to ignore its *Bestial* Trait. It receives +2d10 *Fellowship*.

**Drive:** The animal is trained to pull a coach, cart, plough, buggy, or similar.

**Entertain:** The animal is trained to entertain others. It adds a +10 bonus to appropriate Entertain, Perform, or Play Tests.

**Fetch:** The animal is trained to fetch. This is normally reserved for dogs and similar.

**Guard:** The animal is trained to stay in one place or prowl around as a guard, granting it the *Territorial* Trait.

**Home:** The animal is trained to return home if it is released or lost.

**Magic:** The animal is trained to ignore *Skittish* when it comes to magic, which is required for most mounts used by spellcasters.

**Mount:** The animal accepts a rider. Some creatures are especially belligerent, and will not accept a rider without the correct skill. For example, to ride a griffon, you need the *Ride (Griffon)* Skill.

**War:** The animal is trained for war, gaining +10 *Weapon Skill*. It can also ignore *Skittish* for loud noises.

#### **Undead**

The undead are neither living nor dead, meaning they are not reliant on the usual prerequisites for life such as air, food, water, and so on. This Trait most commonly comes into use when spells, Miracles, or other abilities affect *Undead* only.

# **Unstable**

The creature's corpus is maintained by foul magics that are inherently unstable in the material realm. Whenever they end a Round Engaged with any opponents who possess Momentum when they do not, the creature is driven back, and the magics holding them together weaken and the creature loses 2 Wounds. If the creature ever reaches 0 Wounds, the magics holding them in place collapse, and they are destroyed or driven back from whence they came.

### **Vampiric**

The creature feeds on blood and draws great physical strength from this act. Whenever they perform a successful Bite attack against an appropriate opponent, they heal as many Wounds as the opponent loses. Drinking blood in this way is the *only* way they can heal.

# **Venom (Difficulty)**

The creature's attacks are poisoned or envenomed. When they cause Wounds, their opponent gains a *Poisoned* Condition. Some poisons are more potent than others, and the listed Difficulty should apply to Tests made to recover from the *Poisoned* Condition. See page 186.

### **Vomit**

Under duress, the creature can spew a stream of corrosive spew, dowsing their opponents in foul, semi-digested filth. If the creature starts their Turn engaged in combat, they can give up their Move and Action to activate their *Vomit*. The attacker performs an Opposed Ballistic Skill/Dodge Test against all targets within 2 yards (the attacker's single roll opposed by each individual target). The Test is Easy (+4 SL) for the vomiting creature, due to the close range, and Challenging (+0 SL) for opponents. All losing targets suffer a hit with TB +4 Damage and receive a *Stunned* condition. All weapons and armour carried by affected targets suffer 1 Damage as stomach acid corrodes them away. A creature may only *Vomit* once every 12 hours.

# **Ward (Rating)**

Perhaps because they are magical, wear a special talisman, or are just plain lucky, some blows just seem to miss. Roll 1d10 after any blow is received: if the roll is Rating or higher, the blow is ignored, even if it is a Critical.

### **Wallcrawler**

The creature can effortlessly scale vertical surfaces and even traverse ceilings, ready to drop on unwary prey. They move at full Movement across any appropriate surface and automatically pass all Climb Tests.

# **Web (Rating)**

The creature can create webbing to trap unwary foes. Whenever they successfully hit, opponents gain 1 *Entangled* Condition, with a Strength of the Rating given. See page 186.
