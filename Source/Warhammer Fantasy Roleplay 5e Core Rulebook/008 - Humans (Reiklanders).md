# <span id="page-25-0" data-folio="26"></span>**HUMANS (REIKLANDERS)**

Reiklanders come from the heart of the Empire and are its most numerous people. They tend to be friendly, open, and optimistic, with the confidence that comes from living in the province that gave birth to Sigmar, the Empire's patron god. To outsiders, however, that confidence often appears as arrogance, and Reiklanders have a reputation for meddling in other people's affairs, convinced they know best.

Like all humans, Reiklanders live shorter lives than the elder species, but make up for it with vigour, versatility, and ambition. Humanity's rise over the last few millennia has seen it become the dominant power in the Old World, yet it also has an unsettling affinity for the Ruinous Powers. More humans succumb to corruption than any other species, leaving many elves and dwarfs to wonder whether humanity's greatest strength may also prove its downfall.

Human civilisations have existed in distant lands for thousands of years, but the Empire's people trace their ancestry to tribes that migrated to the Old World after the disastrous war between the elves and dwarfs. The Unberogen tribe settled the Reikland, and their greatest son, Sigmar, united the tribes to found the Empire. His coronation marks year one of the Imperial Calendar, and he is said to have ascended to godhood 50 years later. Today, temples and shrines to Sigmar stand in every settlement, and most Reiklanders are devout believers in his message of unity.

# **Opinions**

**On Dwarfs…**

*'When High King Ironbeard made a gift of Ghal Maraz to Sigmar it was more than a mere token of gratitude. It was an event that set history in motion. Without the leadership and vision of Sigmar, men and dwarfs would have been swept away through goblin villainy, but Sigmar required the strength and resilience of the dwarfs, and to this day we honour their alliance.'*

— Sabine Heistlenburger, Warrior Priestess of Sigmar

#### **On Halflings…**

*'As a youth, I had in my head not to underestimate the Mootlanders. I reckoned great reserves of spirit and resilience lurked beneath the soft-bellied surface. Now that I've campaigned with them, I can say they're fair foragers and cooks but good for little else. They're as light-fingered as Ranald and fight like snotlings.'*

— Stefan Klopper, Member of Kraust's Free Company

#### **On High Elves…**

*'So we shook hands on it and she said that it was a delight to do business with me and that she was so relieved that trade relations between the elven kingdoms and the Empire were a going thing once again. What? I said, I've been dealing with merchants from Ulthuan all my life. Oh, she said, of course, she said, and she got this morose look to her. Strange. Money was good though.'*

— Gustav Jaeger, Altdorf Wool Merchant

#### **On Wood Elves…**

*'The high elves, I find, strike one as haughty and aloof on first acquaintance, and reveal themselves to be increasingly unnerving and sorcerous the more you get to know them. The wood elves are quite different, their conspicuously unnerving and strange exterior conceals an inner core of lofty arrogance that becomes obvious in time.'*

— Jacob Stackeldorf, Professor of Altdorf University

Add one Advance (+5) to any five of the following Skills: Animal Care, Charm, Cool, Evaluate, Gossip, Haggle, Language (Bretonnian), Language (Wastelander), Leadership, Lore (Reikland), Melee (Basic), Ranged (Bow)

*Doomed* and 4 Random Talents

### **Fate**

Humans are destined to play a role in shaping events, and start with 4 Fate.

#### **Fortune**

Humans are smiled upon by Ranald. They start with 3 Fortune.

#### **Movement**

Humans are neither fast nor slow, and have a Movement rating of 4.
