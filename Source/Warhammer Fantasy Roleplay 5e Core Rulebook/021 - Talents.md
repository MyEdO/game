## <span id="page-113-0" data-folio="114"></span>**TALENTS**

You choose your initial Talents as part of creating your Character. Once your adventures have begun, you can buy additional Talents using Experience Points (XP) between adventures (see page 191), usually those from your current Career Level and the levels below it. Some Talents can be taken multiple times, as explained in their descriptions.

### **Accurate Shot**

You are an exceptional shot and know where to shoot an enemy to inflict maximum damage. Your successful Ranged Attacks inflict +1 Damage, or +2 if you took the time to aim (page 167).

### **Acute Sense (Sense)**

One of your primary five senses is highly developed, allowing you to spot what others miss. You may take Perception Tests to detect normally imperceptible details with the associated sense, as dictated by the GM. For example, you might be able to hear the difference between the cry of one wolf and another, or taste the difference between the exact same vintage of wine bottled on two different days. You may take this Talent multiple times, choosing a new sense each time you do so.

### **Aethyric Attunement**

Your experience, talent, or training lets you accumulate magical energy with a reduced risk of miscasts. If you would roll on the **Minor Miscast Table**, you may roll twice and choose whichever result you prefer. You may purchase this Talent a second time to gain the same benefit when rolling on the **Major Miscast Table**.

### **Alley Cat**

You are at home in cities, able to move about without drawing attention to yourself and vanish into any crowd. You gain Advantage on Stealth (Urban) Tests, and can figure out the quickest or least noticeable way to get across any town or city you are familiar with without making a Test.

### **Ambidextrous**

You can use your off-hand far better than most folk, either by training or innate talent. You do not suffer the –2 SL penalty to Tests relying solely on your secondary hand, including attacks made with weapons held in that hand.

### **Animal Affinity**

Wild animals feel comfortable in your presence, treating you much as they would an otherwise innocuous part of the environment. As long as you do not act aggressively towards them, wild animals will not ordinarily attack you.

### <span id="page-114-0" data-folio="115"></span>**Arcane Magic (Lore)**

You either study one of the eight Colour Lores of Magic (Beasts, Death, Fire, Heavens, Life, Light, Metal, or Shadows), a Witch Lore such as Hedgecraft or Witchcraft, or a Dark Lore like Daemonology or Necromancy. Upon learning the Talent, you gain one Spell of your choice from your chosen Lore. You may now memorise additional Spells from your chosen Lore for the following cost in XP.

You may normally learn only one Arcane Lore of Magic. Dark Lores are an exception: depending on your path of study (and with the GM's permission), you may learn a single Dark Lore in addition to one of the other Arcane Lores. You can unlearn this Talent at a cost of 100 XP, but you will immediately lose all Spells linked to the Talent if you do so.

### **Argumentative**

You are especially skilled at drawing others into a quagmire of prolonged and frequently pointless argument. When you argue with someone, they must pass a Cool Test, opposed by your Charm, to disengage from a pointless debate with you. While arguing they are distracted, suffering a -3 SL penalty to most Tests unrelated to you, unless a more important issue is brought to their attention, or they are attacked.

### **Artistic (Art)**

You have a natural talent for art. You may always advance the Art Skill, even if it is not part of your current Career. When you choose this Talent, pick one form of written or representative art, such as painting or poetry. You gain Advantage on Art Tests when working in that medium. You may purchase this Talent more than once, choosing another art form in which to excel.

### **Attractive**

Whether it is your piercing eyes or the flash of your perfect teeth, you know how to make the best use of what the gods gave you. When you successfully use Charm to influence those attracted to you, you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die.

### **Battle Rage**

You are always on the edge of self-control, ready to slip into a violent *Frenzy* at any moment (see page 184). If another ability would allow you to enter a state of *Frenzy*, you may do so without making a Test. Furthermore, when you exit the *Frenzy*  state, you do not incur a *Fatigued* Condition.

### **Beat Blade**

You are trained to make sharp, controlled blows to your opponent's weapon, creating an opening for an attack. Upon a successful Melee Attack, you may choose to forgo inflicting any Damage. If you do so, your opponent may not Oppose further Melee Tests with any Melee Skill until the end of their next Turn, and Melee Attacks made against them gain the benefit of Advantage. They may continue to oppose attacks with the Dodge Skill. This Talent is of no use if your opponent has a larger Size than you (see page 360).

### **Beneath Notice**

You are adept at blending into situations that involve your social betters, fading into the background by showing deference to those who think themselves above you. As long as you are properly attired and make some effort to fit in, most people will simply assume you are meant to be there. Gate guards and the like may ask your business, but any plausible explanation is sufficient for them to grant you passage. This Talent affords no protection if you do something to draw attention to yourself such as fumbling a plate full of drinking cups or brandishing a weapon.

### **Berserk Charge**

You hurl yourself at your enemies with reckless abandon, using the force of your charge to add weight to your strike. When you Charge, add +2 Damage to your strike.

### **Blather**

You are adept at offering effortless, entirely false justifications for being somewhere you shouldn't be, or doing something you shouldn't be doing. When you are caught, make a Charm Test, Opposed by your target's Cool. If you pass, your preposterous explanation convinces them for one Turn (or minute, depending on the situation) per SL you achieved on the Opposed Test. If you achieved a number of SL greater than your target's Willpower Bonus, they remain convinced until someone else points out the incongruity in your explanation.

### <span id="page-115-0" data-folio="116"></span>**Bless (Deity)**

You are watched over by one of the gods and can empower simple prayers. Upon learning the Talent, you manifest all the Blessings of the deity you worship.

Under normal circumstances, you may not learn more than one *Bless (Deity)* Talent. Further, you may not learn the Channelling Skill or the *Arcane Magic*, *Chaos Magic*, *Magic Resistance*, *Petty Magic*, or *Witch!* Talents when you have the *Bless* Talent, and vice versa. You can unlearn this Talent for 100 XP, but will lose all of your Blessings if you do so, and may garner the disfavour of the god you have spurned, with effects determined by your GM.

### **Bookish**

You are an avid reader, and know how to quickly navigate your way around a library. You gain Advantage on any Research Test you make.

### **Break and Enter**

Just by observing a building, locked room, or vault, you can determine what looks to be the fastest and quietest route in. Additionally, you have Advantage on Pick Lock Tests, or Strength Tests made to force open doors or windows.

### **Briber**

You need only share a few words with someone to know if they are open to a bribe, and exactly how much would be required to have them turn a blind eye, or even assist in some nefarious activity. You have Advantage on any Bribery Test you make.

### **Cardsharp**

You are used to playing, and winning, at games, although your methods *may* involve a little cheating. When making an Opposed Gamble Test, if you roll a double, you may reroll your opponent's Test. This additionally negates any benefit your opponent may have gained with Sleight of Hand.

### **Careful Strike**

You are skilled at hitting your enemy exactly where you want to. When making a Melee Attack, the penalty for making a Called Shot is reduced to -1 SL. If your attack is made with Advantage, you may make a Called Shot at no penalty.

#### **Carouser**

You are a seasoned drinker and know how to party hard. You do not suffer any ill effects from consuming modest amounts of alcohol, and only need to make a Consume Alcohol Test if drinking to excess — for example, if attempting to impress a bar full of dwarfs. You gain Advantage on any Consume Alcohol Test you make, as well as any Endurance Test you make to resist the effects of poison.

### **Catfall**

You are as nimble and balanced as a cat. Whenever you fall, it counts as a deliberate fall, allowing you to make an Athletics Test to reduce any Damage incurred. You also gain Advantage on Athletics Tests made while falling.

### **Cat-tongued**

Like Ranald the Trickster God, you blend truth and lies as if there were no difference. You gain Advantage on any Charm Test you make to lie convincingly.

### **Chaos Magic (Lore)**

By accident or design, you have lost a portion of your soul to one of the Dark Gods, and can now practise the foul magics of Chaos. Your ruinous patron immediately grants you access to Spells from the chosen Chaos Lore. Upon learning the Talent, you gain one Spell of your choice from your chosen Lore. You may now memorise additional Spells from your chosen Lore for the following cost in XP.

Each time you obtain a Spell of your chosen Lore, you gain a Corruption Point as the spell infiltrates your mind, never to be forgotten.

Under normal circumstances, you may not learn more than one Lore of Chaos. Unlike the *Arcane Lore* Talent, once learned, this Talent may never be unlearned.

### **Combat Aware**

You are used to scanning the battlefield to make snap decisions informed by the shifting tides of war. You may take a **Challenging (+0 SL) Perception** Test to avoid gaining the *Surprised* Condition. If the GM would normally allow a Test to ignore Surprise in this circumstance, you automatically succeed.

### **Combat Master**

You are adept at making your presence felt on the battlefield, using your size or speed to corner your opponents. You count as two people for the purposes of outnumbering an opponent, or when determining if your side is outnumbered. See page 167 for the rules for outnumbering.

### <span id="page-116-0" data-folio="117"></span>**Combat Reflexes**

You react quickly to danger, leaping to attack your enemy without pause. When you roll for Combat Initiative, roll twice and choose your prefered result.

### **Commanding Presence**

Your presence inspires awe and admiration. In the absence of recognised authority, most people naturally look to you for leadership in a crisis and follow any reasonable orders you give. In addition, you gain Advantage on Leadership Tests.

### **Concoct**

You are skilled at making potions and draughts on the go. You may make a free *Crafting* Endeavour each session to use Trade (Apothecary, Herbalist, or Poisoner) without the need for a Workshop. Trade Tools and raw materials are still required. Due to the rushed nature of the creation, it expires at the start of the next Downtime, regardless of whether the item has been completed or not.

### **Contortionist**

You can bend and manipulate your body in a myriad of seemingly unnatural ways. This allows you to squeeze through gaps that would normally be difficult or impossible, at the GM's discretion. You gain Advantage on any Athletics Test made to escape from bonds.

### **Coolheaded**

Gain +5 Willpower. This does not count toward Advances.

### **Crack the Whip**

You know how to get the most out of your animals. Assuming you have the Drive Skill, you can direct your vehicle's animals to take an Action, as well as a Move, without a Test. When a Drive Test is required, make it with Advantage. When driving a vehicle in a Pursuit, add +1 to the animals' Movement Attributes.

### **Craftsman (Trade)**

You are a true creative talent, sufficiently skilled in a craft to have the potential to elevate it to an art form. Choose a Trade Specialisation. You gain Advantage on Tests of this skill, and may Advance it even if it is not part of your current Career. You may purchase this Talent more than once, choosing a new Trade Specialisation each time you do.

### **Criminal**

You are an active criminal making money from illegal sources, and you are not always quiet about it. When you successfully use the *Income* Endeavour to earn coin from a criminal enterprise of any kind, double the resulting coin.

Whether through distinctive tattoos, shifty looks, or a rough demeanour, your criminal nature is hard to hide. Law enforcers are naturally suspicious of you and your motives, while other criminals recognise you as a fellow scoundrel. You cannot enter a Career whose Career Path lacks the *Criminal* Talent. With GM consent, you may spend 100 XP to remove the *Criminal* Talent.

### **Deadeye Shot**

You always hit an opponent right between the eyes, or wherever else you intend to hit. When making a Ranged Attack, the penalty for making a Called Shot is reduced to -1 SL, or removed entirely if you are making an aimed shot (page 167).

### **Dealmaker**

You are a skilled businessman who knows how to close a deal. You gain Advantage on any Haggle Test you make, and usually have a good idea of when an offer really is final.

### **Detect Artefact**

You are able to sense when magic lies within an artefact. You can automatically tell if an item contains magical energy merely by touching it. If you also possess the *Second Sight* Talent, you may attempt an Intuition Test to determine the exact powers, means of activation, and number of charges the item possesses.

### <span id="page-117-0" data-folio="118"></span>**Dicer**

You are a gaming master, so skilled and lucky in most games of chance that you are frequently accused of cheating, despite rarely needing to. You gain Advantage on any Gamble Test you make.

### **Dirty Fighting**

You have been taught all the dirty tricks of unarmed combat. If you make a Called Shot to the body with an unarmed strike and deal any Wounds, you may choose to sacrifice the normal Damage to inflict the *Low Blow!* Critical Wound (see page 175).

### **Disarm**

You are able to disarm an opponent with a careful flick of the wrist or a well-aimed blow to the hand. Upon a successful melee strike as the attacker, you may choose to sacrifice the Damage. If you do so, your opponent loses a held weapon, which flies 1d10 feet in a random direction. If you wish, you may lose Momentum to pluck the weapon from the air with a flourish. This Talent is of no use if your opponent fights unarmed, is securely attached to their weapon, or has a larger Size than you (see page 360).

### **Distract**

You are trained in simple movements to distract or startle your opponent, drawing eyes from your true intent. Upon a successful Melee Attack, you may choose to sacrifice the Damage. If you do so, your opponent loses Momentum and cannot regain it, or make any Test with Advantage for any other reason, until the end of your next Turn.

### **Doomed**

At the age of 10, you underwent the Dooming, a coming-of-age ritual observed by many humans in the Old World. During the incense-laden rite, a priest of Morr known as a doomsayer foretold the manner of your death. In conjunction with your GM, devise a suitable Doom. If your Character dies in the manner foretold, all allied Characters immediately refresh their Fortune, and your next Character begins play with an additional Fate Point.

### <span id="page-118-0" data-folio="119"></span>**Drilled**

You have been trained to fight shoulder-to-shoulder with other soldiers as a single unit. Allies may freely Disengage from an opponent you are also Engaged with as you cover their retreat. Additionally, on your Turn you may lose Momentum in order to grant Momentum to any allies you are standing shoulder to shoulder with, so long as they also have this Talent.

### **Dual Wielder**

You have a knack for fighting with a weapon in each hand. When fighting with two weapons (see page 167), you do not have to lose Momentum in order to make a free attack with a second weapon. Additionally, when fighting with two weapons, Daggers, Knives, and similar weapons you wield gain the *Parry* Quality.

### **Embezzle**

You are skilled at skimming money from your employers without being detected. When Earning during play or performing an *Income* Endeavour, you can embezzle funds, by using Bribery or Evaluate in place of your normal Earning Skill.

If you do this, a successful Test indicates that you have doubled your money. On a failure, irregularities are discovered and you have to spend time and resources covering up your misdeeds: you earn no money whatsoever.

### **Enclosed Fighter**

You have learned to make the most benefit out of fighting in enclosed spaces. You ignore penalties to Melee Tests caused by confined spaces such as tunnels, the frontline, small fighting pits, and similar. You can also use the Dodge Skill even if it would normally be disallowed due to lack of space.

### **Etiquette (Social Group)**

You can blend in socially with the chosen group, so long as you are dressed and acting appropriately. Furthermore, the GM may warn your Character if they begin to indulge in behaviour considered inappropriate by the social group. Example social groups include: Criminals, Cultists, Guilders, Nobles, and Soldiers.

### **Fast Hands**

You can move your hands with surprising dexterity. You can use Sleight of Hand to entertain people by making small objects vanish or appear. You gain Advantage on Sleight of Hand Tests, as well as Melee (Brawling) Tests made to touch an opponent, such as those required by some spells.

#### **Fast Shot**

You can draw and fire a pistol with lightning speed, attacking before the Combat Initiative sequence begins. If you are not Engaged, you may make a single attack with a loaded weapon with the *Pistol* Trait before the first turn of the Round. This attack uses your Action for the Round. If multiple Characters use *Fast Shot*, resolve their attacks in Combat Initiative order. When your turn arrives, you act as normal, but cannot make another attack.

### **Fearless (Enemy)**

You are either brave or crazy enough that fear of certain enemies has become a distant memory. When you take this Talent, choose any enemy group, such as Giants, Skeletons, or Trolls. You are immune to fear from enemies in the chosen group. You may acquire this Talent more than once, choosing a new enemy group each time you do so.

### **Feint**

You have trained how to make false attacks in close combat to fool your opponent. When attacking in melee, if you have Advantage and your opponent does not, you can force them to defend with Intuition instead of any other Skill or Characteristic.

### **Field Dressing**

You are used to treating wounds quickly with whatever suitable materials you can find at hand. You make Heal Tests with Advantage, and can easily create basic splints or bandages from ordinary materials.

### **Fisherman**

You are a very capable fisherman and know all the best ways to land fish. Assuming a large enough body of water is available, and you have the right gear such as a rod or net, you are able to fish enough to feed yourself and a number of others equal to your Initiative Bonus. You may secure more fish in addition to this using the normal rules for foraging (see page 152).

### **Flagellant**

You have dedicated your pain to the service of your god, and regular and repeated self-inflicted wounds mean that pain now only incites you to greater feats. You feel no pain and may ignore penalties inflicted by Critical Wounds, other than amputations. Additionally, if another ability or effect allows you to *Frenzy*  (page 184), you may enter a *Frenzy* any time you suffer a Wound without requiring a Test.

### <span id="page-119-0" data-folio="120"></span>**Flee!**

When your life is on the line, you are capable of impressive bursts of speed. If you Disengage from combat, you may immediately make an extra Move to sprint away from your opponent. If you choose to Flee in combat (page 163), you do not gain any *Broken* Conditions. Tests as the Quarry in a Pursuit benefit from +1 SL.

### **Fleet-footed**

You are unusually fast on your feet, and gain +1 to your *Movement* Attribute.

### **Frenzy**

You have a deep well of fury inside you, which you can unleash upon your enemies. Making a successful Willpower Test lets you enter a *Frenzy,* allowing you to make additional attacks, ignore most psychological effects, and inflict additional Damage in melee. The full rules for *Frenzy* are described on page 184.

### **Frightening**

You strike fear into the hearts of your enemies. You gain the *Fear* Trait with a rating of 1, or 2 if you are Large (see page 360). Enemies must pass an Extended Psychology Test, requiring a number of successes equal to your Fear rating, with one Test made each turn. Until they do, they cannot gain Momentum, and you gain Advantage on Melee Attacks against them.

### **Furious Assault**

Your blows follow one another in quick succession, raining down on your opponents with the fury of Ulric. Once per Round, if you hit an opponent in melee, you may immediately lose Momentum to make an extra attack.

### **Gregarious**

You just like talking to other folk, and it seems they like talking to you. You gain Advantage on any Gossip Tests you make, and can usually tell where in a settlement gossip is likely to be exchanged.

### **Gunner**

You are practised in the use of blackpowder weapons, and are able to lessen some of their more obvious dangers. When using a Blackpowder, Engineering, or Explosive weapon, you ignore the *Dangerous* Flaw.

### **Hardy**

When calculating your Wounds total, add your Toughness Bonus an additional time.

### **Hatred (Group)**

You are consumed with hatred toward your foes. You develop *Hatred* for a group. When encountering the subject of your Hatred, you must attempt a Psychology Test or immediately attempt to destroy the hated group by the fastest and most deadly means possible. You gain +1 SL on all Melee and Ranged Tests against the specified group, and are immune to Intimidate and *Fear* (but not *Terror*) caused by them.

Examples you could take include: beastmen, goblins, orcs, monsters, outlaws, Sigmarites, undead, and witches. The full rules for Hatred can be found on page 184.

### **Holy Hatred**

Your prayers drip with the hatred you feel for your blasphemous enemies. When causing Wounds with a Miracle, you may deal +2 Wounds.

### **Holy Visions**

You clearly see the great works of the gods all around you. You automatically know when you enter holy ground, can perceive Blessings, Miracles, and Chaos Magic Spells cast within your line of sight, and can sense divine power in an object with a touch.

### **Hunter's Eye**

You are a skilled hunter and know all the best techniques to find game. When travelling through well-stocked lands, you are automatically assumed to be able to hunt down enough game to feed yourself and a number of others equal to your Initiative Bonus, so long as you have time and the correct equipment. You may secure more food in addition to this using the normal rules for foraging (see page 152).

#### <span id="page-120-0" data-folio="121"></span>**Impassioned Zeal (Cause)**

You have a fervent belief in a certain cause, whether it be political, religious, or philosophical. When you talk about your cause, your words fill with passion and fervent zeal. You gain Advantage on Charm Tests when speaking on these matters. You also find it hard to deny, or prevaricate about the cause, and suffer Disadvantage when lying about or denouncing the subject of your passions.

#### **Implacable**

It takes a lot to finish you off. Any time you would gain one or more *Bleeding* Conditions, you gain one less.

#### **In-fighter**

You are skilled at drawing in close to an opponent and making better use of a shorter weapon. As your Action, you can perform an Opposed Melee Test to attempt to step inside your opponent's weapon length. The winner chooses if combat continues as normal or as 'in-fighting'. During in-fighting, any weapon longer than Short counts as an Improvised Weapon. At the start of their Turn, an opponent may choose to lose Momentum in order to end in-fighting.

#### **Inspiring**

Your rousing words and pleas can turn the tide of battle. You have Advantage when you make Leadership Tests in combat.

#### **Instinctive Diction**

You instinctively understand Magick and are capable of articulating complex phrases rapidly and without error. If you are required to roll on the **Minor Miscast Table**, you may reduce the result by 20. If the final result is less than 01, then no Miscast occurs. If you generate a **Major Miscast** this Talent has no effect.

### **Invoke (Deity)**

You are blessed by one of the gods and can empower their cult's Miracles. Upon learning the Talent, you gain one Miracle of your choice from those available to your patron. You may now memorise additional Miracles from those available to your patron for the following cost in XP.

Under normal circumstances, you may not learn more than one *Invoke (Deity)* Talent. Further, you may not learn the *Channelling* Skill or the *Arcane Magic*, *Chaos Magic*, *Magic Resistance*, *Petty Magic*, or *Witch!* Talents when you have the *Invoke* Talent, and vice versa. You can unlearn this Talent for 100 XP, but will lose all of your Miracles if you do so, and will garner the extreme disfavour of your god, with effects determined by your GM.

#### **Iron Jaw**

You are made of sturdy stuff and can weather even the strongest blows. The first time in an encounter you would gain one or more *Stunned* Conditions, you gain one less.

#### **Iron Will**

You possess an indomitable will, and resist willingly bowing down before another. You gain Advantage on Cool Tests made to resist the influence of others, be it through a Fellowship Test or magical means.

#### **Jump Up**

You move with grace and are hard to keep down. You gain Advantage when making a Perform (Acrobatics) Test to entertain or move quickly outside of combat. Whenever you suffer the *Prone* Condition, you may immediately attempt a **Challenging (+0 SL) Athletics** or **Perform (Acrobatics)** Test to spring back to your feet.

#### **Kingpin**

You have earned an air of respectability despite your nefarious ways. The *Criminal* Talent no longer makes law enforcers suspect you or prevents you from accessing certain Careers. Most other Criminals recognise you as someone to respect — unless they're well prepared and gunning for your position and status.

#### **Lightning Reflexes**

Gain +5 Agility. This does not count toward Advances.

#### **Linguistics**

You have a natural affinity for languages and learn new ones with ease. You gain Advantage on Language Tests. Additionally, Advances in any Language Specialisations always cost 50 XP, no matter how many Advances you have already taken. This Talent applies only to languages used for true communication — its effects do not apply to Language (Magick)*.*

#### <span id="page-121-0" data-folio="122"></span>**Lip Reading**

You can work out what people are saying by watching their lips move. If you have an unobstructed view of the speaker's mouth, you can attempt a **Challenging (+0 SL) Intuition** Test to understand what they are saying.

#### **Luck**

They say when you were born, Ranald smiled. You gain +1 maximum Fortune. If you wish, you may purchase this Talent up to three times, for a maximum of +3 Fortune.

#### **Magic Resistance**

You are resistant to magic. The SL of any Spell that would affect you is reduced by 3. Furthermore, if you have this Talent, you may never learn the Channelling Skill or the *Arcane Magic*, *Bless*, *Chaos Magic*, *Invoke*, *Petty Magic*, or *Witch!* Talents, and vice versa.

#### **Magical Sense**

Your senses are especially sharp, and can detect sudden movements and changes in the Winds of Magic, even if you are not a spellcaster. You immediately become aware of any spellcasting within your Perception in yards, although you cannot discern the nature of the magic and can tell only the rough direction of the source. Additionally, if you have the *Second Sight* Talent, or can otherwise perceive the Winds of Magic, you gain Advantage on any tests made to discern their whims or motions.

#### **Magnum Opus**

You are an undisputed master in your field, able to create works of incredible complexity that inspire awe and envy in your peers. When you take this Talent, you may create a single, extraordinary work of art with one of your Art or Trade Skills. This work is all but unrivalled in your field, a unique piece that will always impress, giving bonuses as determined by the GM, most commonly to Fellowship Tests from those who have witnessed your astounding work. Selling the piece will net you at least ten times its normal value. You may take this Talent multiple times. Each time you do, you create a new masterwork.

#### **Marksman**

Gain +5 Ballistic Skill. This does not count toward Advances.

#### **Master of Disguise**

You are an expert at taking on the appearance and mannerisms of others. With nothing but posture changes, face twisting, and careful use of appropriate clothing, you no longer look like yourself. Furthermore, when you successfully use the Entertain (Acting) Skill, you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die.

#### **Master Orator**

You are skilled at speaking on almost any topic, always sounding clear, concise, and confident — even when discussing topics you are unfamiliar with, or taking positions you do not truly believe. You gain Advantage on Lore (Law) Tests made to argue a case before a magistrate, or similar Lore Tests made to argue an academic, theological, or political point before a crowd.

#### **Master Tradesman (Trade)**

You are a skilled and reliable artisan. When you take this Talent, choose a Trade Specialisation. When you successfully use this Skill, you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die. You may take this Talent more than once, choosing a new Trade Specialisation each time you do.

#### **Menacing**

You have an imposing presence, and most people know to steer clear of you. You gain Advantage on Intimidation Tests, and most sensible thieves, bandits, and pickpockets will choose another target as long as one is available.

#### **Mimic**

You have a good ear for accents and dialects and can reproduce them accurately. You gain Advantage on Entertain (Acting, Comedy) and Perform (Clowning) Tests. Additionally, you may replicate any accent you are exposed to for at least a day, which may help with dealing with locals in an area at the GM's discretion.

<span id="page-122-0" data-folio="123"></span>If you are familiar with an accent, you know if another is attempting to fake it or hide it unless they also have *Mimic*.

#### **Night Vision**

You can see very well in natural darkness. Assuming you have at least a faint source of light (such as moonlight obscured by a Marienburg fog, the glowing eyes of a wight, or a bioluminescent fungus) you can see clearly for 20 yards and can extend the effective illumination distance of any light sources by a further 20 yards.

#### **Nimble-fingered**

Gain +5 Dexterity. This does not count toward Advances.

#### **Noble Blood**

You are either born into the nobility, or otherwise elevated to it by in-game events. Assuming you are dressed appropriately, you are always considered to be at least one Status Tier higher than others unless they also have the *Noble Blood* Talent, where Status is compared as normal. Furthermore, you may enter the Noble Career as if it were within the same Class as your current Career.

#### **Nose for Trouble**

You are used to getting into, and preferably out of, trouble. You gain Advantage on Intuition Tests, and can usually tell where in a town or village you can find the opportunity to gamble, fight, or get stinking drunk.

#### **Numismatics**

You are well versed with the different coinage of the Old World and are adept at determining the value of artefacts from distant lands. You gain Advantage on Lore (Heraldry) Tests to identify the providence of any coin, medal, or flag. When you successfully make an Evaluate Test to determine the value of an item, you may use either the rolled SL or the value of your units die.

#### **Old Salt**

You are an experienced seaman and are very used to sea life. When you successfully use the Row or Sail Skill on the ocean, you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die. You count as two boatmen towards the minimum number of crew to pilot an ocean-going vessel.

#### **Orientation**

You have an instinctive sense of direction. You gain Advantage on Navigation Tests. You also automatically know which direction is north with a glimpse at the stars, trees, or whatever other signs you are familiar with.

#### **Panhandle**

You are a skilled beggar, able to get even the most jaded individual to contribute to your cause. You gain Advantage on Charm Tests while begging, including those made during an *Income* Endeavour. Additionally, you can usually find the best places to find shelter for a safe but uncomfortable night's sleep beneath bridges, near middens, and in other urban locations most folks choose to ignore.

#### **Perfect Pitch**

You have perfect pitch, able to replicate notes perfectly. You can learn a piece of music simply by listening to it once, and gain Advantage on Entertain (Singing) and Play (Any) Tests.

#### **Petty Magic**

You have the spark to cast magic within you and have mastered techniques to control it at a basic level. Upon learning the Talent, you manifest a number of Spells equal to your Willpower Bonus. You can learn additional Petty Magic Spells for the following cost in XP.

Full rules for learning new Spells are provided in **Chapter 8: Magic**. You can unlearn this Talent at a cost of 100 XP, but immediately lose all Spells linked to the Talent if you do so.

#### <span id="page-123-0" data-folio="124"></span>**Pharmacist**

You are highly skilled at pharmacy, better able than most to make pills, unguents, oils, creams, and more. When creating an alchemical remedy (see page 154), you produce an additional dose.

#### **Pilot**

You are skilled at leading vessels through dangerous waters, high winds, or when making haste to escape pirates or wreckers. Gain Advantage on Row and Sail Tests.

#### **Public Speaker**

You are skilled at rousing crowds of the common folk. You know how to gather a crowd with ease and can usually gauge the mood of a group and what might motivate them to take action. You gain Advantage on Charm Tests made to influence groups of people. Once you have Charmed a group in this way, you may make Leadership Tests to influence them in other ways, urging them to action, removing *Broken C*onditions, and so on (see page 169).

#### **Pure Soul**

Your soul is pure, quite resistant to the depredations of Chaos. You may gain double the usual number of Corruption Points before having to Test to resist mutation (see page 188).

#### **Rapid Reload**

You reload ranged weapons with practised ease. When using a weapon with the *Reload* Flaw, reduce the number of the *Reload*  Flaw by 1.

#### **Reaction Strike**

Your fast reactions have allowed you to halt charging enemies in their tracks. When you are Charged, you may attempt a **Challenging (+0 SL) Initiative** Test to gain an immediate free attack against that charger outside the normal Turn sequence. This attack is resolved with whatever weapon you are carrying in your primary hand. You can only make one free attack this way each round.

#### **Read/Write**

The written word remains a mystery to most denizens of the Empire, but you have learned your letters and can read and write in any language you can speak.

#### **Relentless**

When you have your mind set on a target, there is nothing anyone can do to stop you reaching them. If an opponent Disengages from you, you can attempt a **Challenging (+0 SL) Athletics**, **Drive**, or **Ride** Test after their Turn ends. A success provides an extra Move to chase after them and lets you freely Disengage from all other opponents to do so. Further, any Tests to chase as a Pursuer in a Pursuit benefit from +1 SL.

#### **Resistant (Threat)**

Your strong constitution allows you to more readily survive a specific threat. Once per session, before rolling a Test to resist a specific Threat (such as Disease, Magic, Corruption, Poison), you may choose to automatically pass that Test. You gain Advantage on all further Tests to resist that threat. You may take this Talent more than once, picking a new Threat each time.

#### **Resolute**

You stand firm in the face of danger with grim determination. When you gain a Condition, you may retain your Momentum if you pass a **Challenging (+0 SL) Cool** Test.

#### **Reversal**

You are used to desperate combats, able to find opportunities to gain an edge in dire circumstances. In an Opposed Melee Test where you are struck but your Test succeeded, you retain Momentum.

#### **Riposte**

Conforming to 'the best defence is offence', you respond to an incoming attack with a counterstrike of your own. If you win an Opposed Test when defending in melee combat, you cause Damage as if you were the attacker.

#### **River Guide**

You know all the tricks for navigating dangerous rivers. You need not Test to navigate dangerous waters unless the Difficulty is Difficult (-1 SL) or lower. If you also have the appropriate Lore (Local) Skill, you never need to Test — you are assumed to know a safe route.

#### **Robust**

You are as tough as old boots and quickly recover from injury. Whenever you test to heal Wounds you may heal an additional 2 Wounds.

#### <span id="page-124-0" data-folio="125"></span>**Roughrider**

You are at home in the saddle in even the most difficult of circumstances and know how to protect your mount from harm during poor conditions. Assuming you have the Ride Skill, you can direct your mount to take an Action, as well as a Move, without a Ride Test. If a Ride Test is required, you may make it with Advantage. Furthermore, when mounted in a Pursuit, add +1 to the animal's Movement Attribute.

#### **Rover**

You are at home in wild places, moving quickly and quietly through Taal's own realm. You gain Advantage on Stealth (Rural) Tests, and can usually determine the easiest route through an area, as well as where to find game, water, and shelter.

#### **Savant (Lore Specialisation)**

You are exceptionally learned and have a significant degree of specialised knowledge in your field of study. Choose any Lore Specialisation you possess. Tests made using this Lore Specialisation are made with Advantage. You may acquire this Talent more than once, choosing a new Lore Specialisation each time you do so.

#### **Savvy**

Gain +5 Intelligence. This does not count toward Advances.

#### **Scale Sheer Surface**

You are an exceptional climber. You gain Advantage on Climb Tests. Additionally, you can attempt to climb even seemingly impossible surfaces such as sheer fortifications, ice shelves, greased poles, and similar, at a maximum imposed Difficulty of Very Hard (-3 SL).

#### **Schemer**

You are a master of politics, seeing conspiracy around every corner, and are adept at weaselling secrets and rumour from those in the know. When you successfully use Gossip or Lore (Politics), you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die.

#### **Sea Legs**

You are used to the rolling motion of the oceans, and are unlikely to get seasick, even in the worst storms. Under normal conditions, you need never Test to see if you become seasick, and you gain Advantage on Tests to avoid it should the GM require one.

#### **Seasoned Traveller**

You are an inquisitive soul who has travelled far and wide, and quickly pick up all manner of local information. You can always advance the relevant regional Lore (Local) Skill whilst you remain in that region, even if it is not part of your current Career.

#### **Second Sight**

You can perceive the shifting Winds of Magic that course from the Chaos Gates at the poles of the world. You now have the ability to percieve the Winds of Magic (see page 236).

#### **Secret Identity**

You maintain a secret identity that allows you to appear richer, or perhaps poorer, than you actually are. You can adopt an alternate persona with a Career of your choice for a set period of time (an encounter, a day, an entire adventure) without having to Test to maintain the charade, and during this period you are treated as having the Class and Status of the chosen Career.

If you wish, you can even adopt this persona for the purpose of carrying out *Banking* and *Income* Endeavours, however, in the case of an *Income* Endeavour, you must take a **Challenging (+0 SL) Earning** Test with either Entertain (Acting) or the chosen Career's normal Earning Skill for the Earning Test (not the Earning Skill of your actual Career). If you have the *Embezzle* Talent, you can use Bribery or Evaluate instead if you are embezzling. If the Earning Test fails, the ruse is exposed, with consequences determined by the GM.

#### **Shadow**

You are skilled at following people through busy crowds and winding streets without losing your mark. You instinctively know when to abandon a tail before your quarry spots you. If someone you are following succeeds on a Perception Test to spot you, you may either allow them to spot you and continue following them, or lose track of them and remain unseen.

#### **Sharp**

Gain +5 Initiative. This does not count toward Advances.

#### **Sharpshooter**

You can make shots of exceptional accuracy. Ranged Tests you make do not incur any penalties from your target being *Prone* or in cover. You may also perform feats of accuracy with your weapons that most would think impossible, such as cutting a hangman's noose with an arrow or punching a hole in a tossed coin with a pistol shot. Such feats of marksmanship still require a successful Ranged Test, with the GM setting the difficulty.

#### **Shieldsman**

You are skilled at using your shield to control the flow of battle. Once per Round, while carrying a shield, you may lose Momentum to either grant your shield's AP bonus to an adjacent ally who is not carrying a shield, or, if you win an Opposed Melee Test while attacking or defending, push your opponent up to 2 yards directly away and no longer count as Engaged.

#### <span id="page-125-0" data-folio="126"></span>**Sixth Sense**

You get a strange feeling whenever danger is near. If you are about to trigger a trap or fall victim to a hidden danger, such as an ambush, collapsing tunnel, or poisoned glass of wine, you may make a **Challenging (+0 SL) Intuition** Test. On a success, you detect the danger at the last possible moment.

#### **Slayer**

You are undaunted by large enemies and attack them with great gusto. If you Charge a creature whose Size is larger than you, you automatically gain Momentum.

#### **Small**

You are much shorter than most folk in the Old World. Gain *Size (Small)*. The full rules for different Sizes are found in **Chapter 12: Bestiary** on page 360.

#### **Sniper**

Distance is of no import to your shooting skills, and you are just as adept at picking off far away targets as those nearby. You suffer no penalties for shooting at Long Range, and only a -1 SL penalty for shots made at Extreme Range.

#### **Speedreader**

You read books at a voracious pace. A single glance is all you require to read a full page of text, and you can easily make your way through even a lengthy tome in a single day. When you make an Extended Research Test, double the SL added by any successful Test you make.

#### **Sprinter**

You are a swift runner with a good level of physical fitness. You gain Advantage on Athletics Tests to run and jump.

#### **Step Aside**

You are skilled at being where enemy weapons are not. If you successfully use Dodge to defend against an attack, you may move up to 2 yards as you dive away, and no longer count as Engaged. Opponents do not gain a free attack when you do this.

#### **Stone Soup**

You are used to getting by with less, and know how to survive lean times. If a member of the party makes a Foraging Test (see page 152), they automatically locate sufficient food to feed you, over and above the number of Characters fed by the Test result, even if the Test fails.

#### **Stout-hearted**

No matter how bad things get, you always seem to come back for more. The first time in an encounter you gain one or more *Broken* Conditions, you gain one less.

#### <span id="page-126-0" data-folio="127"></span>**Striding Gait (Terrain)**

You are experienced in traversing difficult ground. You ignore all movement penalties when crossing over or through a specified terrain. If engaged in a Pursuit in your chosen terrain, you receive +2 SL to all Tests relating to the Pursuit. Example Terrains are: Coastal, Desert, Rocky, Tundra, Wetland, Woodland. You may take this Talent more than once, choosing a new Terrain each time you do.

#### **Strike Mighty Blow**

You know how to hit hard! You inflict +1 extra Damage with melee weapons, or +2 if your attack was made with Advantage.

#### **Strike to Injure**

You are an expert at striking your enemies' most vulnerable areas. When rolling on a Critical Wound table, roll twice and choose which result you prefer. Further, you ignore the Called Shot penalty when going for a Silent Takedown (see page 135).

#### **Strike to Stun**

You know how to use your weapon to subdue, not kill. You count all Improvised Weapons as having the *Pummel* Quality. When using a *Pummel* weapon, ignore the Called Shot penalty when going for a Silent Takedown (see page 135).

#### **Strong Back**

You have a strong back that is used to hard work. You gain a bonus of +1 SL to any Opposed Strength Test, and the amount of Encumbrance you can carry increases by one. If you wish, you may purchase this Talent a second time, increasing the bonus to +3 SL and the number of additional Encumbrance points to three.

#### **Strong Legs**

You have strong legs able to carry you great distances when you jump. When you successfully use the Athletics Skill to sprint or leap, you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die.

#### **Strong-minded**

You are the epitome of determination and resolve. Once per session, before taking a Test, you may choose to take that Test without any negative modifiers.

#### **Strong Swimmer**

You are an accomplished swimmer, able to fight a strong current and outpace other swimmers. You gain Advantage on Swim Tests, and receive a +2 SL Bonus to swimming Pursuits.

#### **Sturdy**

You have a brawny physique, or are very used to carrying things. When calculating the number of Encumbrance Points you can carry, count your Strength Bonus twice.

#### **Suave**

Gain +5 Fellowship. This does not count toward Advances.

#### **Super Numerate**

You have a gift for calculation and can work out solutions to most mathematical problems with ease, without the need for a quill, paper, or abacus. You gain Advantage on Evaluate Tests, and you can keep track of the passage of time accurately so long as you are conscious.

#### **Supportive**

You know what to say and when to make the most impact upon your superiors. You not only ignore the penalty to using Charm on those of a higher Status Tier, but also gain Advantage on Charm Tests made to ingratiate yourself with those of higher Status.

#### **Sure Shot**

You are skilled in placing your shots exactly where they will do the most damage, tearing through vital organs and finding vulnerabilities in your target's armour. Your Ranged Attacks inflict +1 Damage, and may ignore armour with the *Partial* Flaw. If your weapon has the *Impale* Quality, you can ignore armour with the *Weakpoints* Flaw as well.

#### **Surgery**

You are a surgeon, able to open and close the flesh in order to heal others. When you successfully use the Heal Skill outside of combat, you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die. Furthermore, you can treat any Critical Wound marked as needing Surgery, for effects see pages 172 to 179.

#### **Tenacious**

You are a vigorous sort. The first time in an encounter you would gain one or more *Fatigued* Conditions, you gain one less.

#### **Tinker**

You are somewhat of a Johann-of-all-trades, able to repair almost anything. You count all Trade Skills as Basic when repairing broken items. If you have at least 1 Advance in a Trade Skill, you have Advantage on Tests to repair associated items, and such repairs take half as much time.

#### <span id="page-127-0" data-folio="128"></span>**Tower of Memories**

A recollection technique first instigated by the Cult of Verena, reputedly from elven practices taught by the loremasters of Hoeth, *Tower of Memories* allows you to perfectly recall a significant amount of facts by storing them in an imaginary spire. When you successfully use the Lore Skill, you can choose to either use your rolled SL, or the number rolled on your units die.

#### **Trapper**

You are skilled at using all manner of traps, and know how to make basic snares and pitfalls from whatever comes to hand. You gain Advantage on Set Trap Tests. Additionally, with an hour to prepare, you can craft a small animal trap or covered pit (see page 136).

#### **Trick Rider**

You are capable of amazing feats of agility while in the saddle. You can use any of your Perform Skills and unmodified Dodge Skill when mounted.

#### **Tunnel Fighter**

You are at home in tunnels, sewers, and other underground environments. You gain Advantage on Stealth (Underground) Tests, and can easily tell how far you have travelled underground and roughly how deep beneath the surface you are.

#### **Unshakeable**

You are a jaded veteran who has survived more than one hail of shots from blackpowder weapons. You do not need to Test to resist a *Broken* Condition if shot at by a *Blackpowder* weapon.

#### **Very Resilient**

Gain +5 Toughness. This does not count toward Advances.

Gain +5 Strength. This does not count toward Advances.

#### **War Leader**

Your stern gaze and inspiring words motivate your soldiers to fight on to the end. #### **Warrior Born**

Gain +5 Weapon Skill. This does not count toward Advances.

#### **Waterman**

You are fabulously wealthy and are rarely ever short of coin. #### **Well-prepared**

You are used to anticipating the needs of others, and yourself. Once per session, you may pull a Trapping required for the current situation from your pockets, as long as it is Encumbrance 0, could feasibly have been bought recently, and does not stretch credulity too far. #### **Witch!**

You have learned magic through trial and error. Gain one Spell of your choice from any Colour Lore or the Lore of Witchcraft, even if you lack the appropriate *Arcane Magic* Talent. Possession of this Talent may attract the attention of witch hunters, and complicate matters should you wish to join an established magical tradition. You can spend 100 XP to lose this Talent and all associated Spells.
