# <span id="page-190-0" data-folio="191"></span>**SPENDING XP**

Between adventures, you may spend XP to advance your Characteristics and Skills, acquire new Talents, and advance your Career Level. Your Career Level determines what Characteristics, Skills, and Talents are available to you.

You can also use XP to change Career, see the *Change Career* Endeavour, page 197.

# **Characteristic Advances**

Every Career has an Advance Scheme governing its Characteristic Advances. The Advance Scheme shows your character's 10 Characteristics, 3 marked with h, 1 marked with on a brass background, 1 marked with a on silver, and the last marked on gold.

The three marked h are Characteristics you can advance in the first level of the Career. When you enter the second level of a career, the Characteristic also becomes available, and so on.

The XP cost for Characteristic Advances is shown on the **Advancement XP Costs** table. Each Advance you purchase adds +5 to the associated Characteristic. The cost of purchasing Advances for a Characteristic increases depending on how many times you've already advanced that Characteristic.

*Example: Your Initial Agility is 27 and you purchase your first Agility Advance. Your new Agility is 32. This costs you 125 XP. To increase your Agility by a further +5 to a total of 37 costs an additional 175 XP.*

The cumulative XP cost is the cost required to increase a Characteristic if you have no prior Advances. For example, going from no Advances in Agility to +30 would cost 2,100 XP.

Note: While Weapon Skill and Ballistic Skill contain the word 'Skill', this is a historical artefact — they are Characteristics.

# **Skill Advances**

You can advance all the Skills listed for your Career level and lower. The cost in XP of a Skill Advance is shown in the **Advancement XP Costs** table, and depends on the number of Skill Advances you have already taken in that Skill.

Each Skill Advance adds +5 to your Skill level. XP Costs for Skills increase in the same way as for Characteristics.

*Example: You purchase your first Advance (+5) in Stealth and your Agility was 31, your Stealth would be 36. This costs 50 XP. Increasing this by a further +5 to 41 would cost an additional 75 XP.*

The full rules for how to use your Skills can be found in **Chapter 4: Skills and Talents**. Like Characteristic Advances, there is no upper limit to the number of Skill Advances that can be purchased. # **Purchasing Talents**

You can purchase any of the Talents listed for your current Career level as well as those from lower levels.

The rules for every Talent can be found in **Chapter 4: Skills and Talents**. Each Talent costs 100 XP, and purchasing it grants your Character access to the Talent's special rules. Some Talents can be taken multiple times, for 100 XP each time, as listed in their description.

When you purchase a Talent from your Career, remember to tick one box on the Career Advancement Tracker.

# **Advancing Careers**

The rules for increasing your Career Level, or changing to a new Career, can be found under the *Advance Career* (page 196) and *Change Career* (page 197) Endeavours. Even if you are not using the Endeavour system, these rules still apply.
