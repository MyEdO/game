# <span id="page-308-0" data-folio="309"></span>**FOOD, DRINK, AND LODGING**

Poor quality room and board costs half the listed price, and fine quality costs double the listed price. Consuming poor quality food or drink comes with a 10% risk of exposure to the Galloping Trots (see page 180).

**Ale, keg:** Capacity 3 gallons. Empty kegs can be refilled for 18d.

**Dwarf Ale:** Merchants travel across the Old World to purchase potent dwarf ale, such as from the famous brewery founded by Josef Bugman.A pint of dwarf ale requires a **Very Hard (-30) Consume Alcohol** Test (see Consume Alcohol on page 111), and grants immunity to fear for 1d10 hours.

**Room, Common:** Guests sleeping in common rooms should be wary of thieves.

**Room, Inn:** Accommodates 2 guests. Large rooms cost double the price and accommodate 4 guests.
