# <span id="page-234-0" data-folio="235"></span>**MAGIC RULES**

# **Casting Test**

You can cast Spells you have memorised or those you can read from a grimoire (see page 237). To cast a Spell, make a **Challenging (+0 SL) Language (Magick)** Test.

If the Casting Test is successful, calculate the SL and compare it to the CN of the Spell. If the SL is equal to or greater than the CN, you cast the Spell as explained in the Spell description. If not, the Spell fails.

## **Critical Casting**

If you roll doubles on a successful Casting Test, you cause a Critical Casting. Roll on the **Minor Miscast Table** (page 238). Note that the *Instinctive Diction* Talent may help mitigate the risk of Miscasts. If you Critically Cast, you may choose one of the three extra effects:

- Critical Damage: If the Spell causes Damage, it also inflicts a Critical Wound to the Hit Location struck (or the Body if none is defined).
- Total Power: The Spell is cast. You may add the tens digit of your Casting Test to your SL for the purpose of Overcasting.
- Unstoppable Force: The Spell may not be dispelled (see page 237).

Petty Spells do not cause Critical Casting or a Minor Miscast on a Critical.

### **Fumbled Casting**

If you roll doubles on a failed Casting Test, you Fumble the cast. Roll on the Minor Miscast Table.

### **Duration**

A successfully cast Spell lasts for its Duration. If you wish for a Spell to last for a shorter time than it normally would, you must declare how long the Spell will remain in effect when the Spell is cast. Once the Spell is in effect, it can only be ended before the duration elapses by dispelling it.

### **Magic Missiles**

*Magic missiles* are damage-causing Spells. When a *magic missile* is successfully cast, reverse the dice on the Casting Test to determine where the target is struck using the **Hit Locations** table (page 164). To calculate Damage, add your Willpower Bonus to the Spell's listed Damage. This Damage is reduced by the target's Toughness Bonus and Armour Points as usual.

### **Touch Spells in Combat**

Certain Spells require you to touch the target. To touch a creature who does not wish to be touched, make an Opposed Melee (Brawling) or Sleight of Hand Test (generally Opposed by the target's Dodge or Melee Skill) after making a successful Casting Test. If the Spell is a *magic missile*, reverse the Touch Test to determine the Hit Location struck.

Certain magical items, such as enchanted staffs, allow you to benefit from their reach when casting touch Spells. In this case, you may replace Melee (Brawling) or Sleight of Hand with an appropriate Melee Skill, such as Melee (Polearm) in the case of an enchanted staff. This Test is made purely to see if you touch the target with the item. It is not a separate attack that would inflict Damage in addition to the effects of the Spell.

### **Ingredients**

You may channel your magic through an appropriate ingredient before unleashing your Spell. This protects against Miscasts as the attuned ingredient absorbs the worst of any magical backlash.

To use an ingredient while casting, declare that you are doing so before making the Casting Test. Ingredients reduce Miscast level by one step: reducing a Major Miscast to a Minor one, or reducing a Minor Miscast to no effect.

The ingredient is consumed by the process, even if no Miscast was rolled. Ingredients must be bought for a single Spell and normally cost half the Spell's CN in silver shillings.

### **Spellcasting Limitations**

You must speak or sing to cast Spells. If your voice is inhibited, the GM should increase the Difficulty of Casting Tests by one step.

When Spells provide bonuses or impose penalties, the best bonus and worst penalty is applied from all Spells that affect the target. They do not stack.

Unless otherwise stated, you must have line of sight to your target.

# <span id="page-235-0" data-folio="236"></span>**Channelling**

You can gather the magical energy needed to cast powerful Spells by channelling. To channel magic, you must decide which Wind of Magic you wish to channel and use your Action to make a **Challenging (+0 SL) Channelling** Test.

Each SL gathered through channelling adds to a pool of power. When you make a Casting Test for a spell of the appropriate Lore, you may add the SL gathered through channelling to the SL of your Casting Test. After making a Casting Test, all gathered power is lost. If you stop Channelling for any reason, you may make an **Average (+2 SL) Endurance** Test — if you fail, the power is lost.

#### **Critical and Fumbled Channelling**

Rolling a double on a successful Channelling Test adds an additional Willpower Bonus SL to your pool of power. If you fail and roll doubles, lose 1d10 SL and then roll on the Minor Miscast Table, adding +10 to the result for each SL of power lost.

If you take damage or suffer a Condition while channelling, you must make a Hard (-2 SL) Cool Test or lose 1d10 SL and roll on the Minor Miscast table, adding +10 per SL as above.

# **Second Sight**

The *Second Sight* Talent allows you to perceive the Winds of Magic, and may use it to aid you in Perception, Track, or other Skill Tests where traces of the Winds of Magic might provide clues. A Character with the *Second Sight* Talent cannot simply turn it off, even should they wish to. In areas truly saturated by the Winds of Magic, they suffer Disadvantage on Perception Tests to perceive mundane phenomena.

Such places can be a boon to Wizards who know how to draw upon that magic, however. When a Wizard attempts a Channelling Test in an area where that specific type of power has gathered, they gain Advantage on that Test. The table below lists the various lores of magic, and where the associated power tends to gather.

# **Overcasting**

If a Casting Test generates SL beyond those required to cast a spell, you can spend the remaining SL on Overcasting. You can spend a number of SL equal to your Intelligence Bonus plus Willpower Bonus in this way.

- Overcasting can add additional Range, Area of Effect, Duration, or Targets to the Spell. If the Spell is a *magic missile*, it can also add to the Damage. Spells may have additional options in their descriptions.
- Spells with a Range of 'You' only ever target the spellcaster. Spells with a range of 'Touch' or a duration of 'Instant' or 'Special' may not be extended. Spells with no Area of Effect may not have their area increased.
- Petty Spells cannot be Overcast.

# <span id="page-236-0" data-folio="237"></span>**Spells**

The Spells presented in this book come in four varieties. Petty Spells are simple tricks involving small amounts of magic. Lore Spells are those which are exclusive to a particular tradition of magic. Arcane Spells are additional Lore Spells exclusive to their traditions that have similar effects to Arcane Spells of other traditions. Chaos Spells are practised by those who have sold their souls to Chaos.

### **Memorising Spells**

You can memorise Spells by spending the amount of XP noted in the *Petty Magic*, *Arcane Magic*, and *Chaos Magic* Talents.

## **Grimoires**

Grimoires are books containing instructions on how to cast Spells. A typical grimoire contains notes of four Spells from the same Lore of Magic.

You may cast a Spell from a grimoire if the Spell belongs to a Lore of Magic that you possess. You need both hands free to flick through the pages. You cannot Overcast an unmemorised Spell; you need familiarity to modify it beyond the basics.

# **Malignant Influences**

If you are subject to a Corrupting Influence and make a Casting Test or Channelling Test, there is an additional risk. Any time you would normally roll on the **Minor Miscast Table**, you must roll on the **Major Miscast Table** instead.

Spellcasters do not count as Malignant Influences in regard to their own Casting and Channelling Tests.

# **Using Warpstone**

Some spellcasters prize warpstone as it can be used as a magical fuel. If you have a piece of warpstone, double any SL you generate on Casting Tests or Channelling Tests. Warpstone counts as a Malignant Influence as explained above.

When warpstone is used as a magical fuel, it is gradually consumed. Keep track of how many CN a particular piece of warpstone has provided. One gram of warpstone is consumed for every 20 CN of magical energy it provides.

In the Empire, the cults and various secular authorities take an extremely dim view of the use of warpstone, and those who possess or use it will be considered to be undertaking acts of Witchcraft even if they are licensed by a College of Magic. There is a thriving black market for the stuff if you possess the right contacts, though it is expensive.

While all spellcasters might benefit from warpstone, skaven spellcasters use it as the primary source of their magical power. All Casting Tests and Channelling Tests made by skaven suffer from -4 SL unless they possess a piece of warpstone. The most accomplished skaven grey seers are experts in utilising warpstone, and do not count it as a Malignant Influence, though other skaven spellcasters do.

# **Armour Repels the Winds**

Metal and leather armour can make it harder for a spellcaster to control magical energy due to the fact that they attract *Chamon* (in the case of metal) or *Ghur* (in the case of leather). Spellcasters wearing armour suffer -1 SL to all Casting and Channelling Tests for every Armour Point on the Location with the most armour. Casters using *Chamon* may wear metal armour without penalty, and those using *Ghur* may wear leather armour without penalty. Armour Points granted by Spells do not repel the Spell's Wind.

When creating a creature with the *Spellcaster* Trait or using a Magician Template (see **Chapter 12: Bestiary**), they typically eschew armour in order to make the most of their magical powers. Chaos sorcerers may wear Chaos Armour without penalty. Creatures granted an *Armour* Trait by their own scales or hide do not suffer penalties.

# **Dispelling**

Once per Round, you may attempt to dispel any Spell that targets a creature or point you can see within Willpower yards. Make an Opposed Language (Magick) Test against the spellcaster attempting to cast the Spell. If you win, the Spell is dispelled and has no effect. If the casting spellcaster wins the Opposed Test, use the SL for the Casting Test.

### **Dispelling Persistent Spells**

You may attempt to dispel a Spell with a lasting effect as an Action. Make an **Extended Challenging (+0 SL) Language (Magick)** Test. When your SL reaches the CN of the ongoing Spell, it is dispelled.

Multiple spellcasters attempting to dispel the same Spell make Tests separately. If they know the same Lore of Magic, then they can provide Assistance instead. A spellcaster dispelling their own Spell receives +1 SL to the Language (Magick) Test.

# **Multiple Arcane Lores**

An elf spellcaster may learn a number of Arcane Lores equal to their Willpower Bonus. An elf Character may not purchase a new Arcane Magic Talent until they have learned at least 8 Spells from the previous Lore. Any spellcaster may learn a single Dark Lore in addition to another Lore.

# <span id="page-239-0" data-folio="240"></span>**Petty Spells**

For the few humans blessed — or cursed, depending upon your point of view — with the spark of magic, it generally manifests around puberty, and almost always before 25 summers have passed. The first indications of impending witchery are often little tricks, knacks, cantrips, or similar, showing the wizard-to-be should probably be trained for everyone else's safety.

For elves, this is just a part of growing up, and those with interest in magic are schooled to develop their burgeoning talents. For humans, assuming they avoid being lynched, it likely means years of training as an apprentice to a wizard from one of the Eight Colleges of Magic, after which they should never use the little tricks they learned when young again. But most do.

As Petty Spells are not formally codified, they have many different names. Players are encouraged to devise their own, more characterful names, reflecting their personality.

### **Animal Friend**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** 1 yard **Target:** 1 **Duration:** 1 hour

You make friends with a creature that is smaller than you and possesses the *Bestial* Creature Trait. The animal trusts you completely and regards you as a friend.

#### **Bearings**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** You **Target:** You **Duration:** Instant

You sense the influx of the Winds of Magic from their source. You know which direction north is.

# **Careful Step**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** You

**Target:** You **Duration:** Agility minutes

The magic flowing through your feet ensures any organic matter you tread upon remains undamaged: twigs do not break, grass springs back to its original position, and even delicate flowers are unharmed. Those seeking to use the Track Skill to pursue you through rural terrain suffer a -3 SL penalty to their Tests.

#### **Conserve**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** 1 yard

**Target:** Special **Duration:** Willpower Bonus days

You preserve up to a day's worth of rations. During this time they will not rot, develop mould, or go stale, although they can still be harmed by external factors, such as getting wet or being burned or poisoned.

#### **Dart**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Willpower yards **Target:** 1 **Duration:** Instant

You cause a small dart of magical energy to fly from your fingers. This is a *magic missile* with a Damage of +0.

#### **Dazzle**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Touch

**Target:** 1 **Duration:** Willpower Bonus Rounds

The target gains 1 *Blinded* Condition, and gains 1 *Blinded*  Condition at the start of each Round for the duration of the Spell.

#### **Drain**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Touch **Target:** 1 **Duration:** Instant

You touch your target, draining their life. This counts as a *magic missile* with Damage +0 that ignores Armour Points. You then heal 1 Wound.

#### <span id="page-240-0" data-folio="241"></span>**Eavesdrop**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Initiative yards

**Target:** 1 **Duration:** Willpower Bonus minutes

You can hear what your target says as if you were standing right next to them.

# **Gust**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Willpower yards **Target:** Special **Duration:** Instant

You create a brief gust of wind, strong enough to blow out a candle, cause an open door to slam, or blow a few pages to the floor.

# **Light**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** You

**Target:** You **Duration:** Willpower minutes

You create a small light, roughly equivalent to a torch, which glows from your hand, staff, or some other part of your person. While the Spell is active, you may choose to increase the illumination to that of a lantern, or decrease it to that of a candle, if you pass an **Average (+2 SL) Willpower** Test.

# **Magic Flame**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** You

**Target:** You **Duration:** Willpower Bonus Rounds

You kindle a small flame that flickers to life in the palm of your hand. It will not burn you, but will emit heat and set flammable objects alight, like a natural flame.

# **Marsh Lights**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Willpower yards **Target:** Special **Duration:** Willpower minutes

You create a number of flickering magical lights up to your Intelligence Bonus. They resemble torches or hooded lanterns. Provided they remain within line of sight, for your Action you may control the lights by passing an **Average (+2 SL) Willpower** Test; a success allows you to send the lights moving in any direction. They will move at walking pace in a straight line, passing through any objects (or witnesses) in their path, unless you Test again to change their direction.

#### **Murmured Whisper**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Willpower yards

**Target:** Special **Duration:** Willpower Bonus Rounds

You cast your voice at a point within Willpower yards, regardless of line of sight. Your voice sounds from this point, and all within earshot will hear it.

# **Open Lock**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Touch **Target:** 1 **Duration:** Instant

One non-magical lock you touch opens.

# **Produce Small Animal**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Touch **Target:** Special **Duration:** Instant

You reach into a bag, pocket, or hat, or under a rock, bush or burrow, producing a small animal of a type you would expect to find in the vicinity, such as a rabbit, dove, or rat. If there are no appropriate local animals, the Spell does nothing. The temperament of the animal is not guaranteed.

# **Protection from Rain**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** You

**Target:** You **Duration:** Willpower Bonus hours

You can keep yourself and your possessions dry whatever the weather, unaffected by precipitation. This affects rain, hail, sleet, and snow, and any similar water falling from the heavens, but not standing water.

# **Purify Water**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** 1 yard **Target:** Special **Duration:** Instant

You purify all water within a receptacle, such as a water flask, stein, or jug. All non-magical impurities, such as poison or contaminants are removed, leaving crisp, clear, potable water. If the vessel contains another liquid that is predominantly water — such as ale or wine — this is also purified, turning into delicious, pure, non-alcoholic water.

#### <span id="page-241-0" data-folio="242"></span>**Rot**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** 1 yard **Target:** Special **Duration:** Instant

You cause a roughly fist-sized volume of organic material to immediately rot. Foodstuffs perish, clothes crumble, leathers shrivel (losing 1 Armour Point on 1 Hit Location), and similar, as dictated by the GM.

# **Shock**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Touch **Target:** 1 **Duration:** Instant

Your target receives 1 *Stunned* Condition.

#### **Sleep**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Touch

**Target:** 1 **Duration:** Willpower Bonus Rounds

You touch the target, sending them into a deep sleep. If the target has the *Prone* Condition, they gain the *Unconscious*  Condition as they fall asleep. They remain unconscious for the duration, although loud noises or being moved or jostled will awaken them instantly. If your targets are standing or sitting when affected, they start themselves awake as they hit the ground, gaining the *Prone* Condition, but remaining conscious.

If your target is not resisting, and is suitably tired, they will pass into a deep and restful sleep.

# **Sly Hands**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** You **Target:** You **Duration:** Instant

You teleport a small object — no bigger than your fist — from about your person into your hand.

# **Sounds**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Willpower yards

**Target:** Special **Duration:** Willpower Bonus Rounds

You create small noises nearby. You can create quiet, indistinct noises that sound as if they come from a specific location within range, regardless of line of sight. The noises can evoke something specific, such as footsteps, whispers, or the howl of an animal, but nothing so distinct that it might convey a message. While the Spell is active, you may control the sounds by passing an **Average (+2 SL) Willpower** Test. A success allows you to move the sounds to another point within range, or to increase or decrease their volume.

# **Spring**

# **Arcane Spells**

Treat Arcane Spells as extra options for every Lore of Magic, including Witch, Dark, and Chaos Lores. They are counted as Lore Spells in all ways, meaning they get the Lore Attributes of Lore Spells, and can only be learned from and taught to those sharing the same *Arcane Magic* or *Chaos Magic* Talent.

How these Spells manifest in practice will depend on the Lore of Magic you use. For instance, a spellcaster using the Lore of Fire's *Drop* may cause the object to overheat, while a practitioner of the Lore of Shadows may make it slightly insubstantial, causing the object to literally slip between the target's fingers.

#### **Breath**

**CN:** 6 **Range:** Special **Target:** Special **Duration:** Instant

Choose a target within 20 + Willpower Bonus yards and make a **Challenging (+0 SL) Ballistic Skill** Test. The target, and any creature within Initiative Bonus yards of them, may oppose your result with a **Dodge** Test. Those who fail to beat your result are struck by this spell.

Breath inflicts Willpower Bonus Damage. The effects of this spell vary depending on the Lore used to cast it.

- **Acid** *(Metal):* Damage is increased by 4. Weapons and armour carried by affected targets each suffer 1 Damage.
- **Cold** *(Beasts, Light, Necromancy):* Damage is increased by 2. Targets gain a *Fatigued* Condition. Until it is removed, their weapons gain the *Slow* Flaw.
- **Electricity** *(Heavens):* Damage is increased by 2. Damage ignores metal armour. Targets gain a *Stunned* Condition.
- **Fire** *(Fire, Daemonology, Waaagh!, and Tzeentch Chaos Lores):* Damage is increased by 5. Targets gain an *Ablaze*  Condition.

- **Poison** *(Death, Life, Witchcraft, Skaven, and Nurgle or Slaanesh Chaos Lores):* Damage is increased by 2. Targets gain a *Poisoned* Condition.
- **Smoke** *(Shadows):* The spell inflicts no Damage. Instead, the affected area fills with smoke, blocking line of sight and causing *Suffocation* (page 169) for Willpower Bonus Rounds.
