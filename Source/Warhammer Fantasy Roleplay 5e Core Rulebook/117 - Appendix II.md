# **Individual Characteristic Advances**

Characteristic and Skill Advances are bought in +5 increments. However, in some cases, a player might want to purchase individual Advances. This might be because they just need a point or two to get to a Characteristic to a round number and thus increase their Characteristic Bonus, or because they prefer frequent modest improvements over larger jumps in ability.

Use the table below to determine the XP cost for individual Advances. Once you switch to this method, you need to have bought a number Advances that is a multiple of 5 if you wish to return to the method presented on page 191.
