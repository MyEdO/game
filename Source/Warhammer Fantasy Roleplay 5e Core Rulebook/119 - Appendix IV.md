# <span id="page-367-0" data-folio="368"></span>**APPENDIX IV**

# **Conditions Reference**

# **Ablaze (page 185)**

You are on fire! At the end of each Round, suffer 1d10 Damage, reduced by your Toughness Bonus and the Armour Points on the least protected Hit Location. Additional *Ablaze* Conditions adds +1 Damage. If this reduces you to 0 Wounds, you suffer a Critical Wound to the Body.

A successful Athletics Test removes 1 *Ablaze* Condition, and one more for each SL after the first.

# **Besmirched (page 185)**

You are covered in effluent or some other foul substances. Until you have a chance to clean yourself, you suffer Disadvantage to all Fellowship Tests. This Condition does not stack.

# **Bleeding (page 185)**

You are bleeding badly. Lose 1 Wound at the end of every Round, ignoring Toughness Bonus and Armour Points. If you reach 0 Wounds, at the end of each Round, make a **Challenging (+0 SL) Endurance** Test to avoid falling *Unconscious*.

If you are *Unconscious*, *Bleeding*, and at 0 Wounds, at the end of each Round roll a d10. If the result is equal to or less than the number of *Bleeding* Conditions you have, you die. If you roll a 10, remove one *Bleeding* Condition. You cannot wake up until all *Bleeding* Conditions are removed.

# **Blinded (page 185)**

You cannot see, and cannot undertake any task that requires sight, such as reading or making Ranged Attacks. You suffer Disadvantage to Melee Attacks and other Tests that rely heavily on sight but do not strictly require it. Melee Attacks against you benefit from Advantage, unless your foe is also *Blinded.* 

One *Blinded* Condition is removed at the end of your Turn, unless the source is a poison, injury, or other lasting malaise.

# **Broken (page 185)**

You are terrified and convinced you are going to die. You must use your Move and Action to Flee (page 163) and hide. You suffer a -1 SL penalty to Tests not involving running and hiding.

If you are not Engaged, at the end of each Round, you may attempt a Cool Test. A success removes 1 *Broken* Condition, and one more for each SL after the first. If you spend a full Round hiding out of sight of any enemy, you lose 1 *Broken* Condition.

# **Deafened (page 186)**

You are unable to hear. You cannot understand what other characters are saying unless you can make use of the *Lip Reading*  Talent. Every minute (6 Rounds) you may make a **Challenging (+0 SL) Endurance** Test. If successful, remove 1 *Deafened*  Condition, plus 1 for every SL past the first.

# **Entangled (page 186)**

You are wrapped in something restricting your movement. You may not Move, and all your actions involving movement of any kind suffer a penalty of -1 SL. For your Action, you can remove an *Entangled* Condition if you win an Opposed Strength Test against the source of the entanglement, with each SL after the first removing an extra *Entangled* Condition.

If you have an *Entangled* Condition as a result of being caught up in some material, refer to the **Strength of Materials** Table to decide the Strength to use in an opposed test.

| Material         | Strength |
|------------------|----------|
| Wool             | 10       |

# **Fatigued (page 186)**

You are exhausted or stressed, and in need of rest. You suffer a -1 SL penalty to all Tests. A *Fatigued* Condition is normally removed by rest, a Prayer, or a Spell.

How much rest is required to remove a *Fatigued* Condition depends on what caused it. *Fatigued* Conditions caused by being encumbered (page 299), disease, exposure, or other ongoing effect are removed once that circumstance or effect ends.

# **Poisoned (page 186)**

You have been poisoned. While *Poisoned*, you suffer a -1 SL penalty to all Tests and lose 1 Wound at the end of each Round, ignoring Toughness Bonus and Armour Points. If you reach 0 Wounds, you cannot heal until all *Poisoned* Conditions are removed. If you fall *Unconscious* when *Poisoned*, make an Endurance Test every Toughness Bonus Rounds or die horribly.

<span id="page-368-0" data-folio="369"></span>At the end of each Round, you may attempt an Endurance Test. A success removes 1 *Poisoned* Condition, and one more for each SL after the first. A successful Heal Test made by you or another Character removes 1 *Poisoned* Condition, and one more for each SL after the first.

### **Prone (page 186)**

You have fallen to the ground. On your Turn, your Move can only be used to stand up or crawl at half your normal Walking pace in yards. You suffer Disadvantage to Tests involving movement, and Melee Attacks against you benefit from Advantage.

*Prone* does not stack — you are either *Prone* or you are not. You lose the *Prone* Condition when you stand up.

# **Stunned (page 186)**

You have been struck about the head or otherwise disorientated. Your Movement is halved and you suffer a -1 SL penalty to all Tests. At the end of each Round, you may attempt a **Challenging (+0 SL) Endurance** Test to remove 1 *Stunned* Condition, plus one more for each SL after the first. Once all are removed, gain 1 *Fatigued* Condition if you do not already have one. If you possess more *Stunned* Conditions than your Toughness Bonus, gain the *Unconscious* Condition.

# **Surprised (page 187)**

You have been caught unawares. You can take no Action or Move on your Turn and cannot defend yourself in Opposed Tests. Any opponent trying to strike you in melee gains a bonus of +2 SL.

At the end of each Round, or after the first attempt to attack you, you lose the *Surprised* Condition. The *Surprised* Condition does not stack.

# **Unconscious (page 187)**

You are knocked out, asleep, or otherwise insensible. You can do nothing on your Turn and are completely unaware of your surroundings. You are Helpless (see page 167).

The *Unconscious* Condition does not stack. When you become *Unconscious,* lose all *Stunned* Conditions.

In most cases, you wake up after a few hours of sleep, though a loud noise or other event might wake you early. If you are *Unconscious* due to a condition or circumstance, such as *Bleeding*  or certain Injuries (page 176), you wake once the effect that caused you to fall unconscious ends.

If you spend Fortune to remove *Unconscious* but have not resolved the cause of the incapacitation, you fall *Unconscious*  again at the end of the Round.
