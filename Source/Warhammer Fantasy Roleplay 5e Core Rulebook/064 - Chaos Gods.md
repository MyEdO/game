# <span id="page-216-0" data-folio="217"></span>**CHAOS GODS**

The Ruinous Powers of Chaos are the foremost existential threat to the Old World, but much about them remains a mystery. Merely seeking such knowledge is punishable by death without permission from one of the cults, and seeking such permission attracts intense scrutiny of your person and motives, and is seldom granted.

To those learned priests who know of them, the Ruinous Powers are the manifestation of sin itself, a warning that all must live within the moral bounds laid down by the cults. Over indulgence in violence, lust, slovenliness, or unseemly curiosity has direct repercussions for the individual (as they are corrupted and come to a sticky end) and for their community, as their deviant behaviour attracts the destructive attention of these evil forces.

The average person knows nothing of these gods, while those few privy to the dark secret of their existence know them only by euphemistic titles such as the Blood God, the Plague Lord, the Lord of Change, and the Prince of Pain. Even the foolish, doomed souls who study this forbidden lore can only guess what motivates these obscene powers, or whether they possess any agenda beyond their primal impulses.

The little that is known of the Ruinous Powers suggests they are divided, and are as much enemies to each other as to the rest of the Old World. On the rare occasions when they cooperate, much like they did during the Great War Against Chaos over two hundred years ago, the world trembles. Persistent rumours speak of hidden cults to dark powers within the Empire, though most dismiss such stories, believing no sane neighbour would turn so far from the light of Sigmar.
