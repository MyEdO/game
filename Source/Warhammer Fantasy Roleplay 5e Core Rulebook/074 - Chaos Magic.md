# <span id="page-258-0" data-folio="259"></span>**CHAOS MAGIC**

Three of the four Great Powers of Chaos have a unique Lore of Magic — only Khorne, the Blood God, has no Lore of his own, abhorring magic as an insult to warrior honour. Three Spells are provided here for each of the primary Chaos Lores, which can be added to provide flavour to your Chaos cultists in addition to Arcane Spells and the Lore of Daemonology.

# **Lore of Nurgle**

The Lore of Nurgle spreads foul disease and filth, mixing bloated excesses of the Jade and Amethyst Winds with whatever corruption comes to hand, then spewing it everywhere.

**Lore Attribute:** Targets of Spells from the Lore of Nurgle become susceptible to infection and disease. While a Spell from the Lore of Nurgle is in effect, any target who suffers a Wound must Test to see if it becomes a *Festering Wound* (see page 180).

#### **Festering Inflammation**

**CN:** 5 **Range:** Willpower Bonus yards

**Target:** 1 **Duration:** Instant

You conjure up septic fluids in the wounds of your opponent. One of the target's Wounds becomes a Festering Wound.

#### **Miasma of Pestilence**

**CN:** 6 **Range:** You

**Target:** AoE (Willpower Bonus yards) **Duration:** Willpower Bonus Rounds

A thin mist gathers around you. All living creatures within the Area of Effect must pass a **Hard (-2 SL) Endurance** Test or suffer from the *Nausea* Symptom (see page 182). Those dedicated to Nurgle, and skaven plague monks, are not affected.

# **Stream of Corruption**

**CN:** 9 **Range:** Special **Target:** Special **Duration:** Instant

Your maw distends horrifically before you vomit forth a foul stream of diseased filth and horror. Immediately make an Unopposed Acid- and Poison-type *Breath* attack (see page 357). It counts as a *magic missile* with a Damage equal to your Toughness Bonus, and it ignores Armour Points. Further, any targets that take more than their TB in Wounds must pass a **Challenging (+0 SL) Endurance** Test or contract *Blood Rot* (see page 179).

# <span id="page-259-0" data-folio="260"></span>**Lore of Slaanesh**

The Lore of Slaanesh revels in torture and excess, all offered to the Prince of Pain and Pleasure for its eternal delight. It is typically wrought by twisting together the Grey, Gold, and Jade Winds into something perverse and exotic.

**Lore Attribute:** Targets of Spells from the Lore of Slannesh find themselves alive to sensation and temptation. While a Spell from the Lore of Slannesh is in effect, targets suffer -2 SL to resist *Animosity*, *Hatred*, and Corruption, but gain +2 SL to resist *Fear* and *Terror*.

# **Acquiescence**

**CN:** 5 **Range:** Touch

**Target:** 1 **Duration:** Willpower Bonus Rounds

Your magic pierces deep within your target, flooding their senses with strange, euphoric sensations. At the start of each Round, the target must make a **Challenging (+0 SL) Endurance** Test. If they fail, they lose their Move and Action as they are overwhelmed with blissful sensation.

# **From Pain, Pleasure**

**CN:** 6 **Range:** Touch

**Target:** 1 **Duration:** Willpower Bonus Rounds

You confound the target's senses, transforming pain to pleasure. The target gains the *Painless* Creature Trait (see page 360) for the duration of the Spell. As they endure injuries, they experience a wave of pleasurable sensation. Should they suffer a Critical Wound, they must pass an **Average (+2 SL) Endurance** Test or drop their weapons, standing Helplessly as their enemies rain blows down upon them, lost in the ecstatic rending of their own flesh.

# **Phantasmagoria**

**CN:** 8 **Range:** You

**Target:** AoE (Willpower Bonus yards) **Duration:** Willpower Bonus Rounds

In response to your invocation, unearthly visions of beguiling and fearsome creatures fill the area of effect, giving you and all allies the *Distracting* Creature Trait (see page 358) for the duration of the Spell. Targets do not distract one another.

# **Lore of Tzeentch**

Tzeentch is the Master of Magic, Treachery, and Lies. The Lore of Tzeentch epitomises this, as it boasts a multitude of powerful spells focusing upon change, all drawing heavily from the Grey, Gold, Blue, and Bright Winds.

**Lore Attribute:** Those affected by Spells of the Lore of Tzeentch are riven by the transformative magic of Chaos. They must pass a **Corruption** Test or gain +1 Corruption Point. Should they pass, they instead gain +1 Fortune Point, which can be used until the end of the encounter. Such are the whims of Tzeentch.

# **Boon of Tzeentch**

**CN:** 9 **Range:** You

**Target:** You **Duration:** Intelligence Bonus days

The Changer of the Ways twists your mind, granting you access to arcane knowledge. Choose one Spell from any Lore. For the Duration, you may cast it as if you had memorised it, but may not record it in a grimoire. If you channel to cast the new Spell, you may use either Channelling (*Dhar*) or the Channelling Skill appropriate to the Spell, should you possess it. If you suffer a Miscast when casting this new Spell, *Boon of Tzeentch* quits your mind in disgust, and also erases itself from your grimoire should it have allowed itself to be recorded there.

#### **Transformation of Tzeentch**

**CN:** 12 **Range:** Willpower Bonus yards **Target:** 1 **Duration:** Willpower Bonus Rounds

At your command, bonds of aethyric fire coil from the earth, shrouding your target in a cocoon of magical energy. The target gains the *Prone* Condition and is considered Helpless for the duration. At the end of each Round, they must pass a **Challenging (+0 SL) Endurance** Test or gain 1 Corruption Point.

#### **Treason of Tzeentch**

**CN:** 6 **Range:** Willpower yards **Target:** AoE (Initiative Bonus yards)

**Duration:** Intelligence Bonus Rounds

You channel fine strands of traitorous thoughts into your targets' minds, twisting their motivations and fanning fires of discontent. Targets gain *Animosity (Allies)*. Further, unless immune to that Psychology, they cannot transfer Advantage to their allies and, if they score a Critical when attacking, they must pass a **Hard (-2 SL) Cool** Test or target their nearest ally.
