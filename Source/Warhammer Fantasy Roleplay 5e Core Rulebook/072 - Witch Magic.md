# <span id="page-254-0" data-folio="255"></span>**WITCH MAGIC**

The following provides two lists of eight Spells for spellcasters not yet absorbed into the Colleges of Magic, who are almost always on the lookout for witch hunters and Sigmarites.

# **Lore of Hedgecraft**

**Lore Attribute:** The Hedgefolk believe their lore is a gift from the gods, referring to their spellcasters as the blessed few. Due to their ancient traditions and ingrained belief, their Spells cannot be cast without ingredients, which are an integral part of their spellcasting process.

Fortunately, the ingredients they use are easily found and prepared on the fringes of settlements. You receive 1 + SL ingredients on a successful **Average (+2 SL) Lore (Folklore)** Test after 2 hours of work, or you can buy them for 5 brass pennies each.

**Ingredients:** Hedgefolk use easily sourced local materials prepared to exacting standards using special tools. So, ingredients may include things such as the wings of a dragonfly killed with a silver pin, rods of poplar polished with beeswax on Sonnstill, or bones buried beneath a hedgerow for a winter's month.

#### **Goodwill**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** You **Target:** AoE (Fellowship Bonus yards) **Duration:** Willpower Bonus minutes

You create an atmosphere conducive to friendliness and good spirits. All Fellowship Tests within the Area of Effect receive +1 SL, and *Animosity* and *Hatred* Psychologies have no effect.

# **Halétha's Joy**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Touch

**Target:** 1 **Duration:** Fellowship Bonus hours

You plead with a god of fertility to bless the target. While the Spell is active, if biologically possible, they will conceive a child. The Spell ends when used.

#### <span id="page-255-0" data-folio="256"></span>**Mirkride**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** You

**Target:** You **Duration:** Willpower Bonus minutes

Speaking ancient words of magic, your spirit leaves your body, stepping into the Hedge, the dark space between the material world and the spirit realm. For the duration, you stand apart from the world, able to witness it invisibly and completely silently, but not affect it in any way. Your spirit self has the *Ethereal* Trait (see page 358). Your body remains in place, immobile and insensate.

If you take any *Magical* damage, it instead immediately ends the Spell. You can see and interact with spirits, other *Ethereal* creatures, and daemons, even invisible ones, and vice versa.

At the end of the Spell, you are pulled suddenly back to your body. If your body is killed while you are walking the Hedge, your spirit will wander aimlessly for eternity.

#### **Nepenthe**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Touch

**Target:** Special **Duration:** Willpower Bonus hours

As you chant your incantations over a cup, a thick mist issues from your mouth and coalesces as a thick, silver liquid. A character who drinks it within the duration may choose to completely forget one individual, permanently.

#### **Nostrum**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Touch

**Target:** Special **Duration:** Dexterity Bonus hours

You incant a spell over a prepared draught, imbuing it with magical power. If drunk while the Spell is active, the drinker immediately heals Dexterity Bonus Wounds and is cured of one disease of the GM's choice. For every +2 SL, you may cure an extra disease.

# **Part the Branches**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** You

**Target:** You **Duration:** Initiative minutes

Your pupils dilate as you complete your incantation, allowing you to see into the spirit world. This allows you to see all invisible creatures.

# **Protective Charm**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Touch

**Target:** Special **Duration:** Willpower Bonus days

You imbue a charm, such as a small piece of jewellery or pocket-sized trinket, with a spell of protection. Those bearing the charm gain +2 SL to resist magical effects and Corruption.

# **Shepherd's Eye**

**CN:** 0 **Range:** Special **Target:** Special **Duration:** Instant

This spell is most often used to find lost livestock, but is easily put to other uses. You grab a bit of organic matter belonging to a single creature — wool, blood, a piece of their leavings — and add it to a bowl of water. After scrying into the bowl for 10 - IB minutes, secret symbols appear to your sight: the GM will describe the creature's location. If you are familiar with the location, you know where they are.

# **Lore of Witchcraft**

Ingredients for Witchcraft only reduce Miscast level if you roll 01–40 on a Miscast table (keep the roll if moving down to a new table). They are, however, cheap and readily available: herbs and body parts of small animals for the most part. Ingredients cost a Spell's CN in brass pennies to purchase. Alternatively, you may spend 2 hours foraging with an **Average (+2 SL) Lore (Herbs)**, **Outdoor Survival**, or **Set Trap** Test: a success produces 1 + SL ingredients. The Lore of Witchcraft may be used with Channelling (*Dhar*) or Channelling (Magick).

**Ingredients:** Witches use a medley of common herbs and a horrific mixture of animal body parts, often harvested when a creature is still alive. It is not uncommon to find them clutching lizard eyes, dog toes, donkey gizzard, or much, much worse in their bloody hands as they cast their foul magics.

# **Blight**

**CN:** 14 **Range:** Willpower Bonus yards

**Target:** Special **Duration:** Instant

You cause an area to become blighted or desolate. For example you may target either a tavern, well, field, or a domestic animal. If you successfully cast the Spell, the target suffers from a blight:

- All food and drink in a blighted tavern immediately spoils.
- A blighted well becomes instantly brackish and stagnant.
- Any crops currently planted in a blighted field rot overnight.
- A blighted animal sickens. The afflicted beast no longer produces anything (cows produce no milk, coats and hides grow mangy and unusable) and will die in 10 - SL days.

# <span id="page-256-0" data-folio="257"></span>**Creeping Menace**

**CN:** 6 **Range:** Willpower yards

**Target:** 1 **Duration:** Willpower Bonus Rounds

You summon a swarm of creeping, slithering creatures to harass your foes. Each target affected is immediately Engaged by a swarm of Giant Rats, Giant Spiders, or Snakes (your choice of one creature type). Use the standard profiles for the relevant creature type, adding the *Swarm* Trait. For your Action, you may make a **Challenging (+0 SL) Charm Animal** Test to direct one or more swarms to attack a different target. When the Spell ends, any remaining swarms disappear into the shadows.

# **Curse of Crippling Pain**

**CN:** 10 **Range:** Willpower yards

**Target:** 1 **Duration:** Willpower Bonus Rounds

Stabbing a crude representation of your target — a doll or puppet — you inflict crippling pain. When successfully cast, choose which Hit Location to stab:

- **Body**  Target doubles up in agony, gaining +1 *Fatigued*, and must pass a **Hard (-2 SL) Endurance** Test or fall *Prone*.
- **Head**  Target gains a *Stunned* Condition, and must pass an **Average (+2 SL) Endurance** Test or gain -SL additional *Stunned*.
- **Limb**  Limb becomes useless, as if amputated (see page 177).

*Fatigued* and *Stunned* last for the duration. You may stab the doll again to affect an additional, unaffected Location by using an Action to take an **Average (+2 SL) Channelling** Test.

# **Curse of Ill Fortune**

**CN:** 8 **Target:** 1

**Range:** Willpower yards *or* Willpower

Bonus miles (with ingredient)

**Duration:** Willpower Bonus minutes *or* days (with ingredient)

The target suffers bad luck. Laces snap, chairs break, and other minor narrative effects inconvenience them. They suffer -1 SL to all Tests and cannot spend Fortune. If they have no Fortune, they reroll all Test rolls of 01–05. This Spell's ingredient, something belonging to the target (such as a personal possession or strand of hair), lets you ignore line of sight.

# **Glamour**

**CN:** 4 **Range:** Touch

**Target:** 1 adult **Duration:** Willpower Bonus hours

You alter the target's appearance to look however old you wish, within the normal range of adulthood.

# **Haunting Horror**

**CN:** 8 **Range:** Touch

**Target:** Special **Duration:** Willpower days

You target a single location, such as a house or clearing, and inflict haunting dreams and waking nightmares on any who enter there. Those entering while the Spell is active are unnerved by eerie sensations, flitting shadows, and whispered voices lurking on the threshold of hearing. Unless they are witches or their familiars, they gain +1 *Fatigued* Condition. Then, unless they pass a **Challenging (+0 SL) Psychology** Test, they gain another *Fatigued* Condition and a *Broken* Condition, both of which are only removed upon leaving the location.

# **Make Hale**

**CN:** 4 **Range:** Touch **Target:** 1 **Duration:** Instant

Your hands glow with a soothing, pale yellow light and the target relaxes. They heal WPB Wounds and lose 1 *Fatigued* and 1 *Stunned*. With +3 SL, you may change Range to Willpower Bonus yards, causing streams of light to leap from your fingers and twist through the air.

# **The Evil Eye**

**CN:** 6 **Range:** 10 yards (maximum) **Target:** 1 (maximum) **Duration:** Instant

You lock eyes with the target and wither their psyche. Perform an Opposed Intimidate/Cool Test, adding any SL from the Casting Test to your result. If you win, the target suffers *Fear 1* from you and gains 1 *Fatigued* Condition, plus 1 *Fatigued* per +3 SL by which you win. If you win by 6+ SL, they gain 1 *Broken* Condition.
