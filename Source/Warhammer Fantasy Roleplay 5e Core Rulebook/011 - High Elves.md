# <span id="page-31-0" data-folio="32"></span>**HIGH ELVES**

High elves, who call themselves the Asur, are among the oldest civilisations in the world. Proud, cultured, and intensely disciplined, they use ritual and tradition to keep their soaring emotions in check and provide focus for their complicated intellects. They often seem aloof or arrogant to other species, and still harbour disdain for dwarfs after the terrible War of the Beard. Yet not all Asur are solemn nobles. Some revel in adventure, taking to the seas as mariners, traders, and diplomats. In particular, the adventurous 'sea elves' are often found trading with other species, and exploring the world's oceans.

Tall and slender with delicately pointed ears, elves have long, fine hair and melodious voices. Although they appear frail, they are surprisingly strong and extraordinarily agile and dextrous. Male and female elves look much alike, often causing confusion among humans.

Ulthuan, an island continent hundreds of miles west of the Old World, is the homeland of the elves. Its legends tell how the land was formed from the corpse of Indraugnir, father of dragons. Five thousand years before Sigmar, the hero Aenarion passed through the sacred fires of Asuryan, creator god of the elves, becoming the first phoenix king and establishing the twin thrones of Ulthuan with the Everqueen Astarielle. After the death of Caledor II in –1600 IC, the high elves withdrew from the Old World and spent centuries in relative isolation while their society was riven by civil war, the Kinstrife, a subject they seldom discuss with outsiders.

# **Opinions**

#### **On Reiklander Humans…**

*'It's unnerving how quickly they change. I once brokered a deal with a merchant prince in Altdorf. Then, after a brief trade mission to Marienburg, a tour of the Tilean Sea, and a whirlwind romance that distracted me for a while, I returned and embarrassed myself by mistaking him for his father.'*

— Celerian Waverider, Merchant Adventurer from Cothique

#### **On Dwarfs…**

*'Perhaps it is because they are so unlovely to look upon that they have become so unpleasant to deal with. My father told me that, during his youth, he had profitable dealings with dwarf merchants in Barak Varr, prior to all that lamentable squabbling during Caledor's reign. Thousands of years have passed, yet they're still so boorish and bitter about the affair.'*

— Gemariel Stargazer, Scholar from Yvresse

#### **On Halflings…**

*'Apparently, back in the days of our loquacious king, Aethis, a corpulent human emperor condescended to make one of these creatures a decision maker of consequence, and the tradition stands to this day. As if we needed another reason not to place any faith in the people of the Empire.'*

— Lord Valoriel, Highborn Scion of Chrace

#### **On Wood Elves…**

*'Once a delegation from Ulthuan approached Athel Loren only to disappear into the wood, and so we must err on the side of caution when dealing with the Asrai. Those elves of the Laurelorn forest are less forbidding, but one must still take care in assuming them to be allies.'*

— Princess Kaele, Tor Dynal's Envoy to Laurelorn Forest

#### **Names**

Elves use their forename and an epithet translated into Reikspiel when travelling, as they usually consider it a futile task to explain the deeper meanings of their names to non-elves.

**Forenames:** Aesdiaric, Athsoranel, Dorolmor, Farolandril, Gythael, Irandyl, Linmarfin, Mirealiath, Morolric, Ulliadiaellion

**Epithets:** Arrowshaper, Echostrider, Fallowhair, Falconsinger, Foamheart, Goldenhair, Hopestrider, Lightprow, Silverspray, Spellsign

#### **High Elf Physical Characteristics**

**Age:** 30 + 10d10

**Height:** 5'11" + 1d10"

**Eye colour:** Jet, Amethyst, Aquamarine, Sapphire, Turquoise, Emerald, Amber, Copper, Citrine, Gold

**Hair colour:** Silver, White, Pale Blond, Blond, Yellow Blond, Copper Blond, Red Blond, Auburn, Red, Black

## **Fluent Languages**

Take six Advances (+30) in both the Language (Elthárin) and Language (Reikspiel) Skills

## <span id="page-32-0" data-folio="33"></span>**Starting Skills**

Add one Advance (+5) to any five of the following Skills: Cool, Entertain (Sing), Evaluate, Leadership, Melee (Basic), Navigation, Perception, Play (any one), Ranged (Bow), Sail, Swim

#### **Starting Talents**

*Acute Sense (Sight)*, *Coolheaded* or *Savvy*, *Night Vision*, *Second Sight* or *Sixth Sense*, *Read/Write*

#### **Fate**

High elf Characters rarely draw the eye of destiny, starting with 1 Fate.

#### **Fortune**

High elves rely on their own faculties more than luck, starting with 2 Fortune.

#### **Movement**

High elf Characters are especially fast, with a Movement rating of 5.
