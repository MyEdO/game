# <span id="page-204-0" data-folio="205"></span>**THE CULT OF MANANN, GOD OF THE SEA**

**Seat of Power:** Marienburg, the Wasteland

**Head of the Cult:** Camille Dauphina, Matriarch of the Sea

**Primary Orders:** Order of the Albatross, Order of the Mariner

**Major Festivals:** Spring Equinox, Autumn Equinox

**Important Holy Texts:** *The 1000 Shanties*, *Tales of the Albatross*,

*Liber Manaan*

waves

**Common Holy Symbols:** Anchors, five-tined crown, tridents,

The sea god Manann is Lord of the Oceans. Like his element, he is volatile, powerful, and fierce, but capable of beneficence, tenderness, and even playfulness. During his dark moods his rages can be terrible, but when calm he provides sustenance for his worshippers and brings them safely to port. Priests of the Cult of Manann admire his strength and revere him to placate his moods.

#### **Worshippers**

There is no formal hierarchy of the cult, although the matriarch of the sea at Marienburg's Cathedral of Manann is nominally regarded as supreme priestess. There is no central authority or dogma — just a core of common belief amongst his priests and worshippers. Mutual respect and alliance is more important to the cult than formal relationships between temples. People ask priests to intercede with Manann on their behalf when they want smooth passage on a journey, safety for loved ones, trade and food from the sea, and protection from seaborne threats. Manann's clerics usually wear robes of dark greenish-blue or blue-grey, trimmed with a white wave-pattern.

# **Holy Sites**

The cult is prevalent in coastal regions and communities relying on the sea. It is most influential in Marienburg, where it is related to trade, diplomacy, and survival. The Cathedral of Manann, a huge, lavishly decorated complex, is found there. The cult has a prominent role in Nordland, but is less significant in landlocked provinces of the Empire.

#### **Penances**

Penances from Manann often involve hazardous, maritime pilgrimages, tests of sailing skills, or expeditions against the sea god's enemies, especially followers of the heretical Cult of Stromfels, god of predators.

#### **Strictures**

- No whistling or swearing when at sea or on holy ground.
- Never harm an albatross.
- First catch to Manann.
- A silver and fish to every Manannite temple and shrine approached.
- Hunt down the servants of Stromfels wherever they may hide.
