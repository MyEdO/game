# <span id="page-37-0" data-folio="38"></span>**4. SKILLS**

Your Skills are used frequently in the game to determine if you succeed when you are trying to do something. Rolling equal to or under the target number based on your Skill gives success, so the higher your Skill number the better.

**A Skill's starting value is equal to its associated Characteristic. Each Advance increases a Skill by +5.**

Your starting Skill Advances come from your Species and Career:

- Assign the Advances listed for your Species (pages 26–35).
- Assign **8 Advances** among the Career Skills you selected in step 2 (page 36).

<span id="page-38-0" data-folio="39"></span>II You may assign more than one Advance to the same Skill, but no Skill can receive more than **3 Advances (+15)** during Character Creation.

When you have allocated all your Advances, complete the Skills section of your Character Sheet. Record the value of each associated Characteristic, including any Characteristic Advances (you'll buy these with XP during play), then add any Skill Advances to determine the Skill's total value.

**Example:** *Athletics is based on Agility (Ag). If your Ag is 37 after any Characteristic Advances, and you assign 2 Advances (+10) to Athletics, your final Athletics Skill is 47.*

# **TYPES OF SKILLS**

If the Skills aren't printed on your sheet, it means they are Advanced or Group Skills, which you must write onto the sheet in the box provided.

- ***Basic Skills*** can be used by any Character, even without Advances in the Skill
- ***Advanced Skills*** can only be used by Characters with an Advance in the Skill
- ***Group Skills*** cover broad areas, and you choose a specialization when you gain Advances, for example Lore (Empire) or Lore (Tilea)
