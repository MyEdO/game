# <span id="page-29-0" data-folio="30"></span>**HALFLINGS**

Halflings are cheerful, sociable, and possessed of enormous appetites in all things. They care more for good food and strong drink than matters of politics or war, and are notorious across the Empire as thieves, poachers, and scoundrels. Halflings regard unattended property as fair game and see little shame in taking advantage of the careless. Most are quick with a joke, quicker with an excuse, and quicker still with someone else's coin. Even so, they are fiercely loyal to their kin, and few are more generous when surrounded by family, friends, and a well-stocked table.

They are short, apple-cheeked and beardless, resembling big-eyed, round-faced (and round-bodied) human children. Their harmless appearance and easy smiles encourage others to underestimate them — a misconception halflings happily cultivate. Many a traveller has discovered, too late, that the friendly halfling offering directions, sharing a pipe, or buying the first round has picked their pockets while doing so.

The first records of halflings come from the fertile lands where the Blue Reach meets the Aver, around 200 years before the birth of Sigmar. Most still dwell there today, in an area named The Moot after their fondness for settling matters through garrulous mass assembly. The Elder of the Moot, currently Hisme Stoutheart, is custodian of the Haffenlyver, an ancient embroidered scroll recording the lineages of the great halfling clans. They can be found throughout the Empire, however, and Haffenstadt, a district of Altdorf, is packed with halfling families supporting restaurants, taverns, pipeweed stores, and an astonishing number of street-food hawkers. Halflings in more rural areas tend to make their living by running inns, poaching game, or smuggling illicit cargoes.

# **Opinions**

#### **On Reiklander Humans…**

*'What I don't like is the assumption most of them have that I'm either here to cook the meal, wash the dishes, or steal the cutlery. That's annoying, but we've much to thank the Reiklanders for. They've kept us secure, for the most part, down the ages, and even let Hisme voice his opinion on who gets to be the next emperor. Can't say fairer than that.'*

#### **On Dwarfs…**

*'My aunt Bessi was the greediest, as were six of her sons. But Bessi has nothing on them dwarfs. Eyes grow as big as Mannslieb at even a scratch of gold. But, if you tell them that, they'll growl at you like you've taken the last honeycake and start scribbling notes in one of their damned books about insulting their family honour or whatnot.'*

— 'Lilly' Joseppinalina Hayfoot, Pedlar in the Reikland

— Juniper Buttermere, Head Cook at Castle Grauenburg

#### **On High Elves…**

*'I've seen them on the rivers in their white ships. How do they keep them so clean? And their hair… oh, their hair… it's like sunshine, it is. Snuck on a boat once to see what they do. Same as the rest of us, it seems, just more... earnestly.'*

— 'Dainty' Cordelineth Brandysnap IV, Thief from Altdorf

#### **On Wood Elves…**

*'Basically, there are two kindreds, as they call them. The ones in the forest up in Nordland are uncanny, more spirit than flesh, magically attuned to the fey spirits that haunt the woods, wild, capricious, immortal beings that struggle to relate to the mundane lives of us humble folk. As for the ones in the forest in Bretonnia, well now, they're really odd.'*

— 'Jammy' Mercimaus Alderflower II, Scholar from Fielbach

### <span id="page-30-0" data-folio="31"></span>**Names**

**Halfling Forenames:** Antinnerra (Anni), Augustus (Gus), Esmerelda (Esme), Ferdinand (Fred), Heironymus (Hiro), Maximilian (Max), Theodosius (Theo), Thomasina (Tina), Seralinadara (Lin), Valentina (Val)

**Halfling Clan Names:** Brandysnap, Croop, Greenthistle, Fumbletoe, Hamfist, Lostpocket, Lowhaven, Rootbottom, Stoutbarrel, Thorncobble, Willowpot

### **Halfling Physical Characteristics**

**Age:** 15 + 5d10

**Height:** 3'1" + 1d10"

**Eye colour:** Light Grey, Grey, Pale Blue, Blue, Green, Hazel, Brown, Copper, Dark Brown

**Hair colour:** Grey, Flaxen, Russet, Honey, Chestnut, Ginger, Mustard, Almond, Chocolate, Liquorice

#### **Fluent Languages**

Take six Advances (+30) in both the Language (Haffennaff ) and Language (Reikspiel) Skills

#### **Starting Skills**

Add one Advance (+5) to any five of the following Skills: Charm, Consume Alcohol, Dodge, Gamble, Haggle, Intuition, Lore (Reikland), Perception, Sleight of Hand, Stealth (Any), Trade (Cook)

#### **Starting Talents**

*Acute Sense (Taste)*, *Night Vision*, *Resistance (Chaos)*, 2 Random Talents (page 23)

#### **Fate**

Halflings care little for destiny. They start with 2 Fate.

#### **Fortune**

Halflings enjoy a fair share of luck, starting with 3 Fortune.

#### **Movement**

Halflings have short legs and broad frames. They have a Movement rating of 3.
